# Sage Nightbloom

## Role
Botanist and defender of [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md)

## Description
A botanist residing in The Groveborne. Knowledgeable about plants, herbs, and potions.

## Background
Fought against the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) when they threatened The Groveborne.

## Relationship to Party
The party has traded plants found on their journeys with Sage Nightbloom in exchange for potions.
