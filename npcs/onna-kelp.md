# Onna Kelp

## Description
First mate aboard **[The Thornbill](locations/the-thornbill.md)**, [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)'s cog. Met by the party during their voyage east from [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) toward [Forden](locations/forden.md).

## Notes
- Serves as Merrin's second-in-command; trades off with her at the **helm/tiller**
- **Drew the cutaway map of The Thornbill** the party has (signed "O.K.")
- Like Merrin, **not a Penance Crusader** — part of the independent crew of The Thornbill
- **Quite familiar with [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md)** — knows the town well; a useful guide/source on arrival (layout, people, where to find things, possibly the [Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md))
- Personality, background, and disposition toward the party still to be established
