# Calef

## Status
Found by the party in the deeper reaches of [The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md).

## Description
Former Groveborn Ranger. Left the Rangers of his own accord — tired of risking his life. Now estranged from [The Grovekeeper](npcs/the-grovekeeper.md) and living in the deeper reaches of [The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md).

## Location
[The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md)

## Background
Experienced the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) during his time as a Ranger with The Groveborn.

## Connection to the Party
Directed the party to find [Reif](npcs/reif.md). Advised the party to watch for [The Lost Scar](locations/the-lost-scar.md) - the last place he saw the Circle of the Occult. Refused to guide the party further but provided information about their route south.
