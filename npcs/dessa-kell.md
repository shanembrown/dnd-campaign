# Dessa Kell

## Description
The **harbormaster of [Palewater](locations/palewater.md)** (identified by [Onna Kelp](npcs/onna-kelp.md)). Silenced along with the rest of the town in the **mass ritual**; her **Echo** was anchored in the **mirror** in her office.

## Notes
- Her ledger (routine ship traffic) had a **quill frozen mid-word**, dated ~1 day before the party arrived — key evidence the silencing was **instantaneous and simultaneous.**
- Her worn canvas harbormaster's coat bore a **strikingly fine, ornate brass ship pin** — **non-magical** (Arcana), read as a valued personal token. (Note the recurring *brass* motif — cf. the [Avowed Brass Keys](items/avowed-brass-keys.md) — though this pin is unenchanted.)
- **Her Echo was destroyed, not freed:** the party fought it and shattered the mirror-anchor *before* learning her name (contrast [Corum](npcs/corum.md) and [Hen](npcs/hen.md), who were **freed** by naming). On defeat it dissolved into salt that vanished. A lesson: **get the name first.**

## Status — DEAD (confirmed)
**Dessa did not come back.** During the **Breaking** at the salt pans, she was **among the resisting undead the party fought** — and she is **gone** from the restored town.

- **The cost of the shattered mirror.** Everyone whose Echo was *freed by name* was walked out and restored; Dessa, whose anchor was **destroyed** before her name was known, **rose hostile instead of returning.** The party had to put her down a second time.
- **The rule this establishes:** destroying an Echo is **not** a lesser version of freeing it — it appears to sever the thread back. **Name first, always.** She is the one death in Palewater the party can trace directly to their own hand.
- Her **brass ship pin** and her ledger remain in the harbormaster's office; no one is keeping the port's books.
