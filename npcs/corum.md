# Corum

## Description
The **blacksmith of [Palewater](locations/palewater.md)** (identified by the maker's mark **"Corum"** stamped on a horseshoe at his forge). Silenced with the town; his **Echo** was anchored in the **window** of his forge.

## Notes
- His forge held iron-working tools and was **still warm** (extinguished ~1 day ago) — he was **at the anvil** when the silence hit.
- **Freed, not destroyed:** mid-fight, **Danior spoke his name — "Corum"** — and the Echo **vanished saying "freedom."** This was the party's **discovery of the naming mechanic** (see [Palewater](locations/palewater.md)): speaking a trapped Echo's true name releases it.
