# Inquisitor Krell

> Full name: **Thadric Krell** (per [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)).

## Description
An Inquisitor of the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md), encountered by the party at [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md). He is the one who imprisoned the party in the Stockade.

## Role
- Inquisitor — likely responsible for interrogation, internal investigation, or rooting out heresy/dark magic within the order
- First Crusader the party has spoken with at [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md)

## First Encounter
- Party arrived at [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) after burying [Finn](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) and the betrayed Crusader rider near [Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md)
- Did not believe the party's story
- Sent guards to verify the party's account
- Stripped them of weapons and items and locked them in the Stockade pending verification

## Disposition
- Suspicious / hostile-cautious — treats party as potential threats until proven otherwise
- Loyalty unknown — could be a true-believer Crusader doing his job, or part of the same internal betrayal that killed [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md)

## What Captain Merrin Knows
[Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md) sailed with Thadric Krell **many, many years ago** and offered the party her read on him:
- "He was **stern**. He had a way of **arresting those who had done wrong**."
- **Overly zealous** — a true believer.
- *"He may not be your friend, but he may not be your enemy."* — i.e., he's a hardliner who follows the rules and his conscience, not necessarily malicious. Potentially a **neutral-to-reachable** figure rather than a committed antagonist.
- This nuance matters given the keep's schism: a by-the-book zealot like Krell might be **opposed to** the betrayers who killed Istren Vale, not aligned with them — or he could be rigidly enforcing whoever now holds authority.

## Theory: Why He Imprisoned the Party (unconfirmed)
A possibility worth exploring — **not yet confirmed**:
- Krell **saw the [Broken Silver Coin](items/broken-silver-coin.md)** the [Keeper](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) gave the party — the half-coin necklace that symbolizes the old, now-defunct alliance between the Groveborne and the Penance Crusaders.
- On seeing it, **his tone changed** and he had them imprisoned.
- The read: the imprisonment may have been **protective custody** — locking them up "for their safety" because that icon marks them as a **threat or target** to someone in/around the keep.
- If true, it reframes the whole Stockade arc: the keys hanging outside the cells and the lax conditions could have been a **deliberate escape route** Krell left open, not keep-wide chaos.
- Still just one hypothesis; could equally have been suspicion or hostility. Worth testing against future evidence.

**Corroboration:** [Sehir Vondrel](npcs/sehir-vondrel.md), telepathically contacting Raker aboard The Thornbill, said Krell is **an honest man**, that he **imprisoned the party for their safety**, and that **he is alone** (isolated/outnumbered within the keep). This supports the protective-custody read — though Sehir is herself an unverified source. If true, Krell is a lone honest man inside a keep that has turned, which would make him a potential ally and a figure in real danger.

## Notes
- Party knows (but Krell may not):
  - [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) is dead, killed in a betrayal by fellow Crusaders
  - The wounded rider who reached them was fleeing that same betrayal
  - Finn was killed by that rider
- Whether the dispatched guards return with confirmation or with orders to silence the party is the central unknown
