# Inquisitor Marrek

> Name spelling unconfirmed ("Marrek").

## Status
**Threat** — a [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) Inquisitor who arrived at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md) hunting the party.

## Description
An **Inquisitor** of the Penance Crusaders (the same rank as [Krell](npcs/inquisitor-krell.md) and the fallen [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)). Arrived at Candlekeep with a Crusader force, demanding to know "**where the visitors from outside Candlekeep are**" and "**has anyone seen them.**"

## Connection to the Party
- Came hunting the party specifically — almost certainly **tipped off by the compromised [Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)**
- Some Avowed **delayed/stalled** him, but he saw through the ruse
- The party **escaped out the back** of the library (down to the port) with **[Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md)**'s help and slipped away by ship before Marrek could find them

## Notes
- Disposition unknown — whether he means the party harm (like a captor) or "protection" (as Krell reportedly did) is unconfirmed. Treated as hostile by default given the party's fugitive status.
- Which Crusader faction he serves (modern order vs. old saints) is unknown
