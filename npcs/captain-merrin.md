# Captain Merrin

## Description
Captain of **[The Thornbill](locations/the-thornbill.md)**, a small cog. Met by the party at the [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) Docks during their escape, and hired to sail them east to [Forden](locations/forden.md).

*(Name per the ship handout signed by Onna Kelp — "Merrin." Earlier notes spelled it "Marin.")*

## Notes
- Agreed to sail the party east — toward [Forden](locations/forden.md), destination [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md) (~2-week voyage)
- Charged **20 gold pieces per person** (80 gp total for the party of four)
- **Not a Penance Crusader.** Independent operator. A bit of a scallywag (opportunistic, loose with the rules), but reads as dependable — the party has a reasonably good impression of her
- Prefers **"not knowing too much"** about her passengers' business — values deniability/discretion over details. Didn't press the party about why they were fleeing the keep
- Willingness to take passengers fleeing the keep during an active alert suggests she's no friend of the keep's current leadership, or simply wants the coin
- Takes the **helm/tiller** herself, trading off with first mate [Onna Kelp](npcs/onna-kelp.md)
- Her cabin (stern, with a window, charts, and books) is where she hosts the party for dinner / serious conversation
- Carries a passenger she keeps separate: **[Sehir Vondrel](npcs/sehir-vondrel.md)**, who lives below decks — "the crew leaves her be"
- **Sailed with [Inquisitor Thadric Krell](npcs/inquisitor-krell.md) many, many years ago** — knows him personally. Describes him as stern, zealous, and rule-bound, but *"he may not be your friend, but he may not be your enemy"* (see Krell's file). Her shared past with a senior Crusader is itself notable
