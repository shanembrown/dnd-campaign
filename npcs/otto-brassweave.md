# Otto Brassweave

> Name spelling unconfirmed ("Otto Brassweave"). Mechanical bird "Wikket/Wicket" spelling TBC.

## Status
Ally — an [Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md) "**tinkerer of knowledge**" who dwells in the **basement of the chapter house** at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md).

## Description
A vastly knowledgeable Avowed — **the most knowledgeable figure the party has met**, beyond Korinvelle or the Keeper. An **artificer/experimenter** who *applies* knowledge rather than merely cataloging it (a basement exception to the order's hands-off philosophy, kindred to [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md)). Carries a **mechanical bird named Wikket** *(spelling TBC)* on his shoulder.

## Connection to the Party
- Korinvelle sent the party to him as a "tinkerer of knowledge"
- Could **spot ties in the party's Abyssal Hollowing document** (not fluent, but able to cross-reference)
- In exchange, asked the party to help **identify three recent acquisitions** (coincidental, unrelated items):
  1. **Obsidian disc** — Hazik identified it as a **corruption of a Penance Crusader purification ritual** object (a Hollowing keystone)
  2. **Bone whistle** — a **paired locator/comms device**: when played, the counterpart instantly knows the player's location
  3. **Brass key** — attuned to a former Avowed Brother, **[Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)**; pairs to a magical lock somewhere
- Suspects **[Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) is the leader of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)** (matches "the one who oversees all")
- Gave Hazik a **mechanical "cooperation box"** — opened only by two people pulling its two handles together (Raker + Danior); inside, a folded note reading **"Yes"** (likely flavor, underscoring the alliance/"stronger together" theme)

## Notes
- Independent of the compromised leadership — a **trustworthy grassroots ally**
- Noted that **Candlekeep has changed since Hazik left** (1+ year ago): doors locked, more strange creatures, people on edge
