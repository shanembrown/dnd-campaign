# Reader Sevrin

> Name spelling unconfirmed ("Sevrin"). Earlier heard as "Reeder Sevinquill" — likely the same person. "Reader" appears to be the title of the Avowed's leader.

## Status
**Compromised** — the leader of [The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md) at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md), secretly aligned with the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md).

## Description
The **Reader** — apparently the **head of the Avowed**. Present (as "Reeder Sevinquill") during the party's audience with **[Magister Korinvelle](npcs/magister-korinvelle.md)**, where he confirmed the Avowed know of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) and hold a well-documented case of the Hollowing ritual.

## The Compromise
- The Avowed have **historically held allegiance to no one** (neutral knowledge-keepers) — but **[Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)** revealed Sevrin is **giving all Avowed information to the Penance Crusaders**
- Because the party **briefed him directly**, their firsthand intel, identities, and intentions may now be in Crusader hands
- Shortly after, **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md)** arrived hunting the party — strongly implying **Sevrin tipped them off**

## Connection to the Party
- A **danger**, not an ally — the party are fugitives from [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md)
- His compromise explains Candlekeep's changed mood: **Crusader omnipresence**, locked doors, people on edge
- **Open question:** which Crusader faction does his pipeline reach — the modern order, or potentially the [Circle](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) itself? (Eldrix's sealed scroll was found *inside* the Avowed's secret vault.)

## Notes
- Treat as hostile intelligence; trust the **grassroots** Avowed instead ([Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md), [Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md), [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md))
