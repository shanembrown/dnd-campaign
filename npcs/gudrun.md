# Gudrun

**Works at [The Warm Ash](locations/the-warm-ash.md)** — an inn **inside [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)**.

**The party has not met her yet.** She is a **name and a destination**, not yet a contact.

## How the Party Got Her Name
At the inn in the town **a day west on the Drover's Road** — where the party was put up free of charge for driving off the **[Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)** ambush at **[The Waystation](locations/the-waystation.md)** — the party was pointed toward **Gudrun** as **someone in Crusivar who might know more about [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**.

## Why She Matters
Ennic is the thread that runs through everything the party is carrying:

- He gave Hazik **[The Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)**
- He warned that **[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md) is compromised**
- He **sent the party to [Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)** in Crusivar
- He gave the riddle **"eight bone pieces; the chain circles around"** that opened the vault
- The **unmarked [Avowed Brass Key](items/avowed-brass-keys.md)** from that vault was made by **the same craftsman** as Ennic's gifts — and **its lock is still unknown**

He was clearly **inside the secret**. Anyone in the capital who knew him is a live lead — possibly the lock for that key.

## What to Ask
- **Ennic by name** — how did she know him? When? As what?
- **The Avowed** — is she connected to them, or just knew a man who was?
- Has anyone **left anything in her keeping**? Does The Warm Ash have an old locked box, room, or cellar?
- **Show her the unmarked key** (carefully — this is the Crusader capital)
- **Halrick Vord** — does she know where he is?

## Cautions
- **Unvetted.** The party's cover broke *somewhere* before the Waystation ambush.
- A name handed over freely by a stranger at a roadside inn is a lead **and** a way to steer someone somewhere.
- This is **Crusivar**. The party is carrying the Ledger, both rites, the sealed "M" scroll, the Deck and the keys, under fake ash marks.
