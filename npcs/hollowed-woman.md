# The Hollowed Woman (Unknown Name)

## Description
A woman imprisoned within [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md). She has **no eyes** and **cannot hear**. Brother [Elrick Vey](npcs/elrick-vey.md) told the party about her — emphasizing that **she was not always this way**.

## Significance
- Strongly suspected to be a victim of **[The Hollowing](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)** — [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)'s signature ritual, in which victims lose their eyes
- The deafness adds a new wrinkle: prior Hollowing reports described eye removal only; this victim has also been deprived of hearing — either the ritual has evolved, or a second layer of magic is at work
- Could be a survivor of **[Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md)** — timing roughly fits the village's abrupt mass abandonment
- Her imprisonment at Crusader Keep raises hard questions:
  - Did the Crusaders **capture** her (to study or protect)?
  - Are the Crusaders **complicit** in The Hollowing now?
  - Or has Mortivar's influence **infiltrated** the modern Crusader leadership?

## Notes
- Name unknown
- **Held at the keep for ~1 week** — recent, but predates the [Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md) abandonment (~2 days ago). She is not a Wicks Hollow survivor; she comes from a separate, earlier event
- Implies Hollowings are happening **regularly and recently** — not isolated incidents
- Mental state, ability to communicate, and degree of awareness all unknown — needs investigation
- May be the most important witness in the keep if she can be communicated with (touch? writing on the palm? telepathy via the [Medallion of Thoughts](items/medallion-of-thoughts.md)?)
- Could be connected to [Danior](pcs/danior.md)'s past — his village was hit by The Hollowing
