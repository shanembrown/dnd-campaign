# Sehir Vondrel

## Description
A woman who lives **below decks** aboard **[The Thornbill](locations/the-thornbill.md)**, in the steerage among the cargo — a pallet of straw, one lamp, a **grey cat**, and a small wooden box on a barrel beside her. The crew **leaves her be**. Discovered by [Das](pcs/das.md) during the voyage from [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) toward [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md) on [Forden](locations/forden.md).

## Notes
- Das **heard her ask for [Raker](pcs/raker.md) by name** — possibly **telepathically / "in his mind"** rather than aloud
- She appeared **unaware of Das's presence** — so the "asking for Raker" wasn't aimed at Das directly; he overheard/intercepted it
- Has a **grey cat** with her
- **Working theory:** the cat may be a **familiar** (Find Familiar grants a telepathic link + lets the caster see through it). That would explain how she's "reaching" for Raker without knowing who's actually in earshot — she may be projecting/scrying via the cat, not targeting Das. Could make her a spellcaster (wizard/warlock) or another druid
- A **small wooden box** sits on the barrel beside her — contents unknown; worth noting
- [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md) clearly knows she's aboard (it's a 5-crew ship and "the crew leaves her be") — so she's a deliberate, tolerated passenger, not a stowaway

## Contact with Raker (below decks, after dinner)
Raker and [Danior](pcs/danior.md) went below; Sehir called to **[Raker](pcs/raker.md)** telepathically again ("Raker" in his head). What she conveyed:
- **She has dreamt of Raker many times** — strongly suggests she's a **seer / dreamer / prophetic figure**, and that Raker matters to whatever she's foreseen
- **On [Inquisitor Thadric Krell](npcs/inquisitor-krell.md):** he is an **honest man**; he imprisoned the party **for their safety**; and **he is alone** (isolated/outnumbered within the keep). This *corroborates* the protective-custody theory
- She **gave Raker a leather-bound box** — opened to reveal **[The Deck of Many Things](items/deck-of-many-things.md)** (13 cards, identified by Hazik). An extraordinary and dangerous gift to hand a seemingly random party — strong evidence her interest in Raker is deliberate and tied to her dreams
- **Raker is "not to speak of her"** — she wants her presence and this exchange kept secret
