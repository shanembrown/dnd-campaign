# Jory Tinwhistle

## Description
A **small, braided grey-haired woman** — proprietor of **The Tinwhistle Travelling Wonder Show**, encountered camped at dusk beside the **Drover's Road**, east of **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)**. Hers was the fire and mumbling the party heard from a distance.

The show is housed in a **mechanical house-wagon that walks on legs rather than wheels**, which she calls **Gertrude**.

**The show carries her own name** — Tinwhistle. So the "whistle" in it is a family name, not a device.

Das and Hazik (both human, and less conspicuous on Forden) approached first while Raker and Danior held back in the treeline.

## Gertrude
She calls the walking wagon **"Gertrude"** — a name, not an *it*.

### Origin — Gertrude Chose Them
**Jory's group did not build or buy Gertrude. They FOUND her several years ago, and she simply started following them.**

- **Gertrude is autonomous.** A walking mechanical house that *decided* to attach itself to a group of travelling performers is not a vehicle — it is something closer to a **companion or a stray**. Nobody drives it; it comes along.
- **GERTRUDE COMMUNICATES.** She **suggested [Danior](pcs/danior.md) press a button** — an intentional, specific prompt directed at a person. This is not an autonomous machine walking a road; it is something that **conveys ideas to people.** (Danior pressed it; **nothing visibly happened** — which fulfils The Oracle's *"a button will betray you within the hour."*) **How does she communicate, and how much does she understand?**
- **She responds to positive affirmation.** Encouragement works on her. That is a **behavioural** response, not a mechanical control — you don't compliment a cart. Whatever Gertrude is, she has something like **morale**.
- **So the maker is unknown, and unaccounted for.** Someone built a walking house, and it ended up wandering loose where a small show could find it. **Where did it come from, and why was it alone?**
- Note this **undercuts the "Tinwhistle = tinker lineage" theory** — the family name may be coincidence, or the show may be named for a craft it no longer practices.

### ⚠️ Gertrude Is NOT Magical
**[Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) ran an Arcana check on Gertrude: as far as he can tell, there is no magic happening.**

This is the strangest fact about her. A house that **walks**, **follows people of its own accord**, **responds to encouragement**, and **suggests ideas to specific individuals** — with **no magic in it at all.**

- That makes Gertrude **technology, not enchantment.** Someone in Illium built an autonomous, communicating machine by **mechanism alone**. That is a different and arguably more alarming kind of capability than a magic item.
- It also means **Identify and Dispel Magic are useless on her** — she cannot be unmade or read by arcane means, and nothing arcane will detect her.
- **Worth running the same check on [The Oracle](npcs/the-oracle.md)** — an owl automaton making *accurate predictions* with no magic would be a far bigger finding than a magical one.

⚠️ **Two independent lines of advanced clockwork now exist in the party's experience:** Gertrude + **[The Oracle](npcs/the-oracle.md)** (an owl automaton, explicitly *not* like Wikket), versus **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)**'s work at Candlekeep. **Ask Jory where she found Gertrude** — the location may matter more than the machine.

- **The obvious opening.** Helping repair Gertrude buys goodwill from someone whose trade is knowing every road and every face between here and [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md) — cheaper and warmer than questioning a stranger cold.
- **Naming the machine** suggests either long attachment or that Gertrude is **more than a machine** (cf. Otto's **Wikket**). Worth watching whether she talks *to* it.
- **RESOLVED — [Danior](pcs/danior.md) got Gertrude unstuck.** The wagon was **stuck rather than broken**; Danior freed her. **This is the party's first deposit of goodwill with Jory** — they proved useful before asking to be carried, which is the right order.

## THE PLAN — Riding the Show into Crusivar ✅ ACCEPTED
**Jory and her group are travelling to [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)** — the party's destination. The party asked to **blend in with the show as performers/crew and enter the city undetected** — and **Jory took them on.**

Sequence that earned it: Das and Hazik (the two humans) approached the fire first; **[Danior](pcs/danior.md) got Gertrude unstuck**; the party pitched their talents; Jory agreed.

**Why it works:**
- **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md) is hunting them**, and the party has been conspicuous all along the Drover's Road (non-human, wrong size for these parts). A **travelling wonder show is the one context where unusual-looking people are expected** — the very thing that exposes them becomes their cover.
- They arrive as **known, expected entertainers with a reason to be there**, not four strangers presenting at the gate of the Crusader capital.
- Establishes a **legitimate reason to move about the city** while hunting **[Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)** and seeking **[Bael Threnfall](npcs/bael-threnfall.md)**.

### Stage Names (assigned by Jory)
| PC | Stage name |

- **These double as aliases.** Entering Crusivar, the party is billed under names **Marrek has never heard**. Use them consistently — a slip in public is the failure case.
- **Lamplighter fits Raker's act exactly:** he's pivoting to **Produce Flame** (a cantrip — unlimited, unlike Wild Shape's 2 uses) for fire-handling. Light in the hand, 10-minute duration, no cost, and it doubles as a weapon nobody in the crowd recognises.
- **"The Titan from the Tundra"** leans on Danior's size — and the **[Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md)** is a real place in Illium, so the billing is half-true.

**Risks to manage:**
- **They must actually pass as a show.** Someone will expect them to *do* something. Worth assigning acts now: Raker (Wild Shape — a beast-handler act that is genuinely real), Hazik (stage magic that isn't stage magic), Das (feats of strength), Danior (?). A performer who can't perform is a hole in the cover.
- **The contraband problem.** They will be carrying the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)**, both **[rites](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)**, **Eldrix's sealed "M" scroll**, the **[Deck of Many Things](items/deck-of-many-things.md)**, and **[Avowed Brass Keys](items/avowed-brass-keys.md)** — into the **Penance Crusader capital**. A search at the gate is the failure case. Consider what's hidden where, and whether Gertrude has a compartment.
- **Jory's group are now exposed too.** If the party is caught, the show is caught harbouring them. They deserve to know what they're agreeing to — and **how much the party tells her is a live choice.**
- **She is not yet vetted.** A showwoman's trade is noticing and remembering people, and she travels everywhere. Warm, useful, and unverified.

## ⚠️ The Show Cannot Fight
Confirmed at the **[Waystation ambush](locations/the-waystation.md)**: **Jory's group are not fighters.** They are performers and road-folk, not a caravan guard.

- **The party won the fight (7 Skeletons + 1 Cult Fanatic), but the show contributed nothing to it** — and would not survive a second ambush without them.
- **This makes the arrangement mutual.** The party gets cover into [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md); **Jory's people get the only protection they have.** They are now genuinely dependent on four strangers they met three days ago.
- **And it makes them hostages to the party's problems.** The [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) attacked a **travelling show at a public waystation** because the party was in it. Everyone in Jory's company is now in danger they did not sign up for and cannot answer.
- **The party owes her the truth.** She agreed to carry performers, not to be a shield for people the Circle *and* [Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md) are hunting. Whether they tell her is the live moral choice — but she cannot make an informed decision about entering Crusivar without it.

## Why She Matters — the Clockwork Thread
- **A walking mechanical house is serious craft.** The only comparable thing the party has met is **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)**, the Avowed's basement "tinkerer of knowledge" at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md), with his mechanical bird **Wikket** — the single most knowledgeable figure the party has encountered, and the one who suspects **[Eldrix](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)** leads the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md). **Worth asking whether she knows Otto, or where such work is made.**
- **"Tinwhistle" is her surname**, so the earlier whistle-flag is likely nothing — but note the family name pairs *tin* with *whistle*, and she travels in a walking machine. A **tinker lineage** is the natural read. Worth asking how far back the wagon goes in her family.
- **A travelling show goes everywhere and talks to everyone.** She has been up and down these roads. She is a potential source on: the **[salt man](npcs/the-salt-man.md)** (tall, salt-associated outsider, went north on this very road), **[Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)**, Crusader movements, the road to **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)**, and **[Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)**.

## Cautions
- **The party stands out** and knows it — they'd been drawing looks all day. A showwoman's trade is *noticing and remembering people*, which cuts both ways.
- **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md) is hunting the party.** Anyone who travels and trades in stories is also someone who could sell one.
- They are **coming from Palewater**, where ~35 people just died. Worth having a consistent account before answering questions.

## Open Questions

- Was she anywhere near **Palewater** before or during the silencing?
- Is she alone? (The party heard **mumbling** — one voice, or several?)
