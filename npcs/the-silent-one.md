# The Silent One

## Description
A **giant** imprisoned in a cell block at [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md). Called "The Silent One" by Crusaders — but only because **no one at the keep speaks Giant**. He is not mute; he simply has had no one to talk to.

## Communication
- [Danior](pcs/danior.md) and [Das](pcs/das.md) both speak Giant and are now able to converse with him
- Major intel opportunity: he has likely been listening to keep activity for however long he's been held, with everyone assuming he couldn't understand them

## Disposition
- **Does not like humans — at all.** Cooperation is not assumed; persuasion and trust-building will be a real challenge
- Reason for the hatred is unknown — could be personal history, the fact of his imprisonment by humans, or general giant-kind hostility
- Implication: human-fronted negotiation is risky; the party may need to lead with non-human members or with respectful Giant-language address that treats him as an equal, not a rescued prisoner

## Notes
- First identified by [Elrick Vey](npcs/elrick-vey.md) only as "The Silent One"
- Encountered during the post-breakout exploration of the second cell block
- Length of imprisonment, reason, and disposition (beyond the human aversion) unknown — currently being established via conversation
