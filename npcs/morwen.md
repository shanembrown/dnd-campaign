# Morwen

## Status
Defeated by the party in the [Stagnant Swamp](locations/stagnant-swamp.md).

## Description
A hag living in a hut/cabin deep in the [Stagnant Swamp](locations/stagnant-swamp.md). Accompanied by her cat, [Mister Scratch](npcs/mister-scratch.md).

## Location
Hut/cabin in the [Stagnant Swamp](locations/stagnant-swamp.md).

## Connection to the Party
Hostile. Attacked the party when encountered in the Stagnant Swamp. Was performing a ritual using the same demonic magic previously encountered at [Featherthorne Hollow](locations/featherthorne-hollow.md). A [Sigil of the Occult](items/sigil-of-the-occult.md) was found at her hut, confirming her connection to the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md).

## Notes
The ritual liquid she was using is capable of turning the dead into undead.
