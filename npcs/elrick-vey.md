# Brother Elrick Vey

## Description
A priest imprisoned in a second cell block within [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md). Devout follower of **"the older saints"** — a traditional strain of faith the modern [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) consider outdated.

## Background
- A priest by vocation
- Holds to the old saints — older religious figures whose teachings the current Crusaders have moved away from
- Was locked up as a **"heretic"** for refusing to stop proclaiming their ways
- Imprisonment suggests the modern Crusader order is now hostile (or at least intolerant) toward its own older traditions

## Significance
- Concrete evidence of a **doctrinal schism inside the Penance Crusaders** — the order has drifted from its older traditions, and is jailing the holdouts
- Aligns with what [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) said: the Crusaders have become too zealous
- May explain the **betrayed rider near [Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md)** — that rider was killed by fellow Crusaders; possibly he was on the "old saints" side of the schism
- May connect to [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md)'s death — Istren was Order of the Sentinel Guards (a related/older order); his murder may be part of the same purge
- May connect to [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)'s fall — he was the order's most zealous member; the order's drift could be tied to his influence
- Potential ally: enemy of the current Crusader leadership, has theological knowledge, possibly knows other dissenters inside the order

## Notes
- Met by the party after the Stockade breakout, in a separate cell block within the keep
- Told the party about the [Hollowed Woman](npcs/hollowed-woman.md) held nearby (~1 week)
- Identified other prisoners on this cell block: a **gnoll**, **"The Silent One"**, and a **"nobleman"**
