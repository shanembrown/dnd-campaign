# Captain Marin

## Description
A ship captain met by the party at the [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) Docks during their escape.

## Notes
- Agreed to sail the party east — toward [Forden](locations/forden.md) and [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)
- Charged **20 gold pieces per person** (80 gp total for the party of four)
- Willingness to take passengers fleeing the keep during an active alert suggests one of:
  - She doesn't care about Crusader politics and just wants the coin
  - She has her own reasons to be no friend of the keep's current leadership
  - She didn't know the party was running from the keep (less likely given the rain, the timing, and the bells)
- Her ship's name, size, crew, and home port are not yet known
- Unknown whether she is honest, opportunistic, a smuggler, or worse — verify before sharing mission details
