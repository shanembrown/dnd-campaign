# Magister Korinvelle

> Name spelling unconfirmed ("Korinvelle").

## Status
A senior **Magister** of [The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md) at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md).

## Description
A senior-ranking Avowed who **welcomed [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) "home."** The welcome was **cool** — the Avowed didn't expect Hazik to return, and disapprove of how he **applies magic** in the world, whereas the order focuses solely on **gathering knowledge, not using it**.

## Connection to the Party
- Received the party's briefing on the rituals, reanimations, and necromancy they've witnessed
- **Referred the party to two specialists:**
  - **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)** — "tinkerer of knowledge," in the chapter-house basement
  - **[Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md)** — scholar (lore/records)
- **[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)** (as "Reeder Sevinquill") was present at the audience

## Notes
- Loyalty unclear — senior leadership at a time when the order's head (Sevrin) is compromised. Treat with caution.
