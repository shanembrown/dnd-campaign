# Klif

## Role
Groveborne Ranger (estranged)

## Location
[The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md) — lives in the deeper reaches. Attended the Ranger Assembly at [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) despite his falling out.

## Description
Former Groveborne Ranger. Left the Rangers of his own accord — tired of risking his life. Now estranged from [The Grovekeeper](npcs/the-grovekeeper.md) and living in the deeper reaches of The Rootpath. Experienced the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) during his time as a Ranger.

## Notable Interactions
- Directed the party to find [Reif](npcs/reif.md) at [Hightower](locations/hightower.md)
- Advised the party to watch for [The Lost Scar](locations/the-lost-scar.md) — the last place he saw the Circle of the Occult
- Refused to guide the party further but provided route info south (swamp is harder than mountains, path through mountains on the far side)
- Called out [Creel](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) during the Ranger Assembly for his suspiciously thin report ("Really Creel? That's it?")
- Has a deeper rift with Creel that the party sensed goes beyond professional disagreement (insight check)
- The Keeper chose not to intervene when Klif challenged Creel

## Notes
- Stood at the back of the room during the assembly, not in the main circle
- Despite being estranged, attended the assembly — suggests the current threat is serious enough to set aside grievances
