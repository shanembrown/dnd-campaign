# The Grey Cat

## Description
A **grey cat** that has appeared to the party across multiple locations — utterly unbothered, watchful, and evidently **more than an ordinary animal.** Not confirmed to be [Sehir Vondrel](npcs/sehir-vondrel.md)'s familiar (she keeps a grey cat and told Raker not to speak of her), but the resemblance is noted.

## Appearances
- **[The Reaver's Tooth](locations/the-reavers-tooth.md)** (Candlekeep port bar)
- **[Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)'s deathbed** (Candlekeep)
- **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)** — sitting on the inn bar, the **only living thing** left in the silenced town

## Notes
- At Palewater, **Speak with Animals** produced **clear, coherent narrative** (not the vague fragments a normal beast gives) — strong evidence it is a special/magical presence:
  - *"They went silent."*
  - *"The tall salt man went back to his house."* (see [the salt man](npcs/the-salt-man.md))
  - *"The fish man walked into the ocean."*
  - Named its **favorite person** in town: **[Hen](npcs/hen.md)** the potboy — which led the party to **free Hen's Echo.** The cat effectively steered the party toward mercy.
- Recurring, benevolent-seeming guide. True nature and connection to Sehir still open.
