# The Oracle

## Description
A **mechanical something** that lives **on a shelf inside Gertrude**, the walking wagon of **[Jory Tinwhistle](npcs/jory-tinwhistle.md)**'s travelling show.

The party became aware of it by **sound** — a persistent **clicking / gear-turning** they heard for a long stretch of the road east toward **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)**, which they initially assumed was Gertrude's legs. On asking, the show's people corrected them: **"that's not Gertrude, that's The Oracle."**

## What It Is
A **small figurine shaped like an owl** — and **a robot / automaton**, not a carving. It sits on a shelf inside Gertrude, **clicking and turning its gears continuously** as the show travels.

- **Confirmed NOT like Wikket.** The party specifically checked against **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)**'s mechanical bird and found it is **a different kind of thing** — so this is *separate* craft, not Otto's work or his tradition. That makes it **more** interesting, not less: there are now **two independent lines of advanced clockwork** in the world.
- **An owl is the scholar's bird** — wisdom, knowledge, watchfulness. Worth noting the party's other knowledge-institution is **[The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md)** at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md).
- **It is a bird that never stops ticking.** Wikket was a companion; this one is a **mechanism left running**. Something is being counted, tracked, or listened for.

## How It Works — Coin-Operated
The owl has a **coin slot.** Feed it a coin, it speaks a fortune.

- **It seemed AWARE of the party when they entered** the wagon — but **not** when they inspected it closely. It notices arrivals; it does not respond to scrutiny.
- Answers appear to be drawn from a **fixed set of eight** (rolled on a **d8** at the table). **The same roll gives the same answer**, which the party confirmed directly.

### Fortunes Received (Drover's Road, en route to Crusivar)
| Coin | Roll | Fortune |
|---|---|---|
| 1st silver | **4** | *"A button will betray you within the hour."* |
| 2nd silver | **4** | *"A button will betray you within the hour."* (identical — same result repeated) |
| 3rd | **7** | *"Rain, not here, somewhere, not here. Definitely rain."* |
| 4th | **2** | *"The tall one sneezes twice."* |
| 5th | **1** | *"You will step in something before moonrise."* |

**Known so far: 4 of 8 fortunes** (results 1, 2, 4, 7).

### ⚠️ IT IS REAL — Fortunes Confirmed
Both testable predictions **came true within the hour**, on exiting Gertrude:

- ✅ **"You will step in something before moonrise."** — **[Raker](pcs/raker.md) stepped in horse dung** stepping out of the wagon.
- ⚠️ **"A button will betray you within the hour."** — **Gertrude suggested [Danior](pcs/danior.md) press a button. He pressed it, and NOTHING HAPPENED.**

**The Oracle is not a novelty.** It is **accurate** — and it delivers real predictions in the register of trivia, which is the perfect disguise for a working prophecy machine. Nobody takes a talking owl seriously when it opens with a sneeze and a rain forecast.

**On the button:** "nothing happened" is not a resolution — it is the *shape* of a betrayal. A button that does nothing has either **already done something elsewhere**, done something **not yet visible**, or **failed to do the thing it was needed for**. Note also that **Gertrude prompted the press.** Whatever the button was for, the suggestion did not come from Danior.

**Reading it:** the repeat on a matching roll proves the answers are **canned, not responsive** — this behaves like a **fairground gimmick**, eight stock fortunes on a wheel. The "rain" answer is pure filler nonsense, which supports that.

**The pattern across four coins:** the fortunes are **small, petty, and near-term** — a button, some rain elsewhere, a sneeze. Nothing grand, nothing about fate or death. Either it is a cheap novelty producing trivia, **or it is a genuinely accurate oracle with an extremely short and unimpressive range.** *"The tall one sneezes twice"* is testable within minutes — **watch Danior.** If he sneezes twice, the owl stops being a toy.

**And:** *"a button will betray you within the hour"* is **oddly specific** for stock patter, and the party is about to walk into the Crusader capital **in costume, under stage names**, carrying contraband. A cheap, plausible, and very literal reading is available — and acting on it costs nothing.

## Jory's Take
After the party fed it several silver, **[Jory](npcs/jory-tinwhistle.md) leaned in and joked: *"I told you I was good at making money."***

- She frames the Oracle as **a money-maker** — which is either an honest showwoman admitting her novelty is a novelty, or a **deflection** from something she'd rather not discuss. Either way it is **charming and disarming**, which is a showwoman's whole craft.
- Worth noting she is **relaxed about the party spending on it** and made no move to stop them or explain it further.

## Why It Matters
- **It runs constantly.** Not wound up for a performance — **clicking away on a shelf, all day, while travelling.** Whatever it is doing, it is doing it continuously and unattended. That is not how a stage prop behaves.
- **The clockwork thread.** This is the **second** piece of serious mechanical craft the party has met, after **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)**'s mechanical bird **Wikket** at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md) — Otto being the most knowledgeable figure the party has encountered, and the one who suspects **[Eldrix](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)** leads the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md). **Ask Jory whether she knows Otto.**
- **The name is a claim.** An "Oracle" purports to *know* or *foretell*. The party is already carrying two prophecy-adjacent things: the **[Deck of Many Things](items/deck-of-many-things.md)** (66 cards, 13 held, none drawn) given secretly to Raker by the seer **[Sehir Vondrel](npcs/sehir-vondrel.md)** — who **dreamt of Raker repeatedly** and told him **not to speak of her**.
- **Is it a show act or a real thing?** A travelling wonder show would obviously bill a fortune-telling machine. That is also perfect cover for one that actually works.

## Cautions
- **If it perceives, it perceives the party** — who are travelling under **stage names** as cover, carrying the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)**, both **[rites](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)**, Eldrix's sealed **"M" scroll**, the **Deck**, and the **[Avowed Brass Keys](items/avowed-brass-keys.md)** into the Crusader capital.
- Raker was told **not to speak of Sehir**. Consulting an oracle is a good way to break that instruction by accident.
