# Brother Ennic

> Name spelling unconfirmed ("Ennic").

## Status
**Dying** — [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md)'s uncle and father-figure, his closest living family. A Brother of [The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md) at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md).

## Description
An elderly **Avowed Brother**, the man who raised/trained [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md). On his deathbed when Hazik returned to Candlekeep. A **grey cat** (the same one seen at **[The Reaver's Tooth](locations/the-reavers-tooth.md)**) slept on his chest during the visit — meaning unknown.

## Connection to the Party
On his deathbed, Ennic gave Hazik a series of crucial revelations and gifts:
- **Saddened** to learn Hazik has chosen the **[Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)** as his "**cross to bear**" — the Circle is widely spoken of, even by a bedridden elder
- **Warning:** the Avowed have **historically held allegiance to no one**, but **[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)** (the order's leader) is **compromised — feeding all Avowed information to the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)**
- Revealed that the keyholder **[Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)** is in **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)**, and **sent the party to seek him out** ("Ennic sent us" as an introduction)
- Gave Hazik **[The Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)** — a family heirloom inherited from the person who raised/trained Ennic; translates any text
- Gave the cryptic phrase **"eight bone pieces; the chain circles around"** *(exact wording TBC)* — which proved to be the solution principle for the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)** vault lock (a closed chain of eight tiles)

## Notes
- Ennic clearly **knew of the secret Hollowing vault** and steered Hazik toward it from his deathbed
- His craftsman-lineage produced both the Reader's Lamp and the **unmarked key on a chain** found in the vault (Hazik recognized the same maker's hand)
- Embodies a thematic contrast: the **true "Reader" legacy** (illumination, knowledge freely given) passes to Hazik even as the institutional Reader, Sevrin, has gone dark
