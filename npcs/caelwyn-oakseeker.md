- Recognized the ancient shrine [Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md) described but was reluctant to share details publicly — said the darkness from the past was "locked up to protect" and they don't want to "open old wounds"
- Revealed that the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) has close ties to the Crusaders — [Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) was one of their most zealous members before falling
