# Istren Vale

## Title
Lightkeeper (title given by the [Order of the Sentinel Guards](factions/order-of-the-sentinel-guards.md))

## Location
[Southreach](locations/southreach.md)

## Notes
- Has witnessed monstrosities in the [Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md)
- Has a divine connection to a "god of light" — can receive guidance through prayer
- Prayed over [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md)'s folio and was told by his god that Greenward is on a quest for good; offered to help the party find Greenward
- Has not seen a Groveborne Ranger in the area — no sign of [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md)
- Old family friend of [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md)'s grandfather, suggesting a historic connection between the Order of the Sentinel Guards and The Groveborne
- Lent the party his boat to sail to [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md)
- When the party returned to [Southreach](locations/southreach.md), Istren seemed "off" — something about his demeanor felt untrustworthy
- Destroyed the Abyssal inscription the party recovered from Xellath — possibly out of zealotry rather than malice
- Was adamant the party should travel with him to [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md)
- [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) noted the Penance Crusaders may have become too zealous, their vision "clouded"
