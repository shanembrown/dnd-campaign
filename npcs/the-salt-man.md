# The Salt Man

## Description
A figure known only from the **[grey cat](npcs/the-grey-cat.md)'s** account at **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)**: a **tall figure associated with salt**, an **outsider who "visits [Palewater] from time to time."** After the town was **silenced** (~1 day before the party arrived), the cat said he **"went back to his house"** — i.e., **left town** for wherever he actually lives.

## Notes
- **Person of interest / possible cause.** A salt-associated outsider, present around the time a whole town was turned to a mass salt-ritual, who comes and goes — a pointed profile in a town that **collects salt from the sea** and was **silenced into the salt pans.**
- Likely the one who **handled the "for E. G. who burned" memorial token** at the [Concordance church](locations/palewater.md) *after* the salt fell (that token was the only one not evenly dusted).
- **Footprints led north out of town** onto the **Drover's Road** — possibly his departure trail.
- Not seen directly. **Do not confuse** with the *"fish man"* (a local netmender who walked into the ocean) — a separate figure.
