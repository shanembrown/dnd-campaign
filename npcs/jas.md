# Jas

## Description
A young boy, roughly 12 years old, who works at [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) — handles the **stables** and helps **train the dogs**.

## Notes
- Encountered by the party after their breakout from the Stockade
- Appears scared and unsure of what is happening in the keep — suggests the chaos/neglect the party has been sensing is real, not staged
- Party was honest with him — admitted they were prisoners and are seeking a way out
- Trains the keep's dogs — likely means the mastiffs/hounds used by patrols (including the one the party killed) are familiar with his voice and scent. Possible asset for slipping past dogs in the halls
- As stable boy, likely knows routes to the stables, which horses are ready, and possibly a back exit via courtyard/mounted-patrol gate
- Unknown whether he has loyalty to the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) or is just employed by them
- Too young to be a combatant; party should consider his safety if a fight breaks out

## Keep Alert Bell
- During conversation with the party, a large distant bell rang **twice**
- Jas explained: the bell signifies the **level of alert** for the keep — two rings means the alert state has **elevated**
- Jas was visibly disturbed by it — implies this is not a routine occurrence
- Likely triggered by: the party's breakout being discovered, Vesker's flight, or some unrelated incident in the keep
