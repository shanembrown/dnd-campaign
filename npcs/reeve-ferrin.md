# Reeve Ferrin

*(Spelling to confirm with DM. Note: **"Reeve" is also a historical title** for a local official/magistrate — so this may be **Ferrin, the Reeve of Palewater** rather than a first name. Worth clarifying, as it changes whether the town has a formal office or just an elder everyone defers to.)*

## Description
A **senior figure / elder** of **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)**, and — in the aftermath of the Breaking — the person the restored town now looks to as its **de facto leader**.

## Why They Matter
- **The harbormaster's chair is empty.** **[Dessa Kell](npcs/dessa-kell.md)** did not come back (her Echo was destroyed rather than freed; she rose among the resisting undead during the Breaking). Palewater lost its most obvious authority, plus the **first 12**, **~20–23** others, and **[Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)** — roughly **35 dead** out of a town of ~180.
- **The Crusaders are no longer trustworthy to them.** Palewater cannot look to the **[Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)** for guidance — the silencing was done by an **Inquisitor-Scholar of that very order**, using a rite stamped with the **Penance cross** and presented as Church-sanctioned mercy. Whatever the town now knows of that, it has cut them loose from the institution that would normally speak for them.
- That vacuum is what elevates Ferrin: **not elected, not appointed — deferred to.**

## Gift to the Party (the day after the Breaking)
Ferrin gave the party provisions from a personal stash **collected over the years**:
- **1 potion he *believes* is a Healing Potion**
- **1 potion he *believes* is a Potion of Invisibility** — replaces the one Danior drank scouting the salt pans
- **A few loaves of bread** and a **waterskin**

⚠️ **Both potions are unverified — "he believes."** An amateur's long-held collection, not an apothecary's labelled stock. Worth an **Identify** or at minimum an **Arcana** check before anyone drinks one mid-fight.

A small gift, but it is what a salt-town elder has to give — and it reads as **thanks**, which tells the party how Ferrin regards them.

## Open Questions
- **How much do they know?** Do they understand that a Crusader did this, that the party undid it, and that ~35 people are gone? What Ferrin believes will shape what the whole town believes.
- **What did the restored experience?** Ferrin is the natural person to ask whether the townsfolk remember the silence, the lost time, or anything of where they were — see **[Hen](npcs/hen.md)**.
- **Who tells them about Dessa?** Ferrin should hear it from the party, along with her **brass ship pin** if they took it. Also: **no one is keeping the port's books.**
- **Is Palewater an ally?** A salt-town on Forden that has just lost faith in the Crusaders is a potential friendly port — and a potential **witness** the party could call on. They can testify that the Hollowing is a Crusader rite *and* that it was reversed.
- Did Ferrin know **Garrow** — how long he'd been in town, who visited him, who gave him the rite?
