# Odd Tom

**Pie vendor** in [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md), working a street cart — **The Ember Pie** (meat pies, mushroom pies, honey cakes, hot and fresh).

## First Meeting
Met **briefly** on the way in from the gate, when **[Danior](pcs/danior.md) stopped to buy pies.** A short, ordinary interaction — no business done beyond the food.

## Why He's Worth Remembering
A **street vendor in a busy market** is one of the best low-risk information sources in a city: he stands in the same spot all day, sees everyone, and talks to strangers for a living. **A performer can ask a pie man questions that would look very strange asked of a Crusader.**

Useful to come back to for: directions, who's been asking after outsiders, what the bells mean, who applies the ash, where **[The Warm Ash](locations/the-warm-ash.md)** is, and local gossip about the cathedral.

## Notes
- **"Odd Tom"** is a nickname someone gave him, which means the city has an opinion about him. *Odd how?*
- First named friendly face the party has inside the capital.
