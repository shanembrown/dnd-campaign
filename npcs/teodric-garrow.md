# Inquisitor-Scholar Teodric Garrow

## Status
**LOST in the tide-breaking at Palewater.** During the attempt to reverse his rite, the **climbing tide flooded the salt pans** and forced the party to abandon the ritual unfinished. **Garrow did not make it out.** Lost alongside the **first 12** at the ritual's center and **~20–23** of the remaining souls. *(No body recovered — a resurfacing remains the DM's option, but the party is treating him as lost.)*

**Turned repentant before he vanished.** After the party showed him Mortivar's merciful **[Rite of the Vessel](items/the-rite-of-the-vessel.md)** and the evidence his own rite was a concealed corruption, Garrow was left in disbelief — *"questioning his entire existence."* He **committed to helping the people** of Palewater, **offered his services**, handed over his **ledger of the 180 names**, and **enchanted Das's shortsword into [Tidehold](items/tidehold.md).** Emotionally fragile but resolved when last seen.

## Description
The **only conscious person** the party has found in the silenced town of **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)**. Introduced himself as **"Inquisitor-Scholar Teodric Garrow."** A **"nutty professor"** type — eccentric, absorbed in his work. He **lit two torches** and hurried inside when he saw the party on the north dyke; then **opened his door as they approached**, urging *"you've seen them, please come in!"*

## Notes
- **"Inquisitor" is a [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) rank** — same title as **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md)** (hunting the party) and **[Inquisitor Krell](npcs/inquisitor-krell.md)** (who proved honest/protective). Alignment **unconfirmed** — could be zealot, defector, or Circle-aligned. The party has NOT tipped their hand about the Ledger, the Avowed, or Eldrix's scroll.
- **Working on "cracking the purification code."** This ties him directly to the campaign's core mystery: the **Hollowing is a corrupted Crusader purification rite**. Garrow is actively studying **purification** — either a lead toward a **cure/reversal** (the party's goal for Draffy / the Hollowed Woman), or he is the one **running/sustaining the experiment** on the town.
- Counts **180 souls** in the ritual behind the party (party's earlier estimate was ~200). His **precise, researcher's count** is a tell — he is **studying** the entranced townsfolk, not horrified by them. Open question: is he the **cause**, a **witness**, or trying to **save/reverse** it?
- **Scholar-eccentric archetype** — compare **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)** (the Avowed "tinkerer of knowledge").

## The Reveal (in his own words)
- He has developed what he believes is a **more "pure/humane" purification** of **"blight-infested souls"** — the **180 people** entranced in the Palewater salt pans. Historically, purification meant **"the pyre"** (burning); his **life's work** has been to find a gentler way. (Likely ties to the Concordance church's **"for E. G. who burned"** memorial token.)
- **He keeps a LEDGER of the 180 names** (offered to the party) — very likely in **processing order**, which supplies both the *names* and the *entry sequence* the reversal needs ("call them out, last-to-first"). Solves the roster bottleneck. Grim mirror of the Circle's 200-year **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)** — same bookkeeping impulse.
- **He has already COMPLETED the rite on the 180 — within the last day.** Because his copy of the **[Rite of Purification](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)** hid the release (Breaking, viii) and left the Sealing (ix) visible, "completing" it means he **very likely performed the Sealing** — believing it the merciful end. **The 180 are probably sealed** (though party observation says they're still alive/savable — a closing window, not a shut door).
- **CRUCIAL — he cannot undo it.** He **openly admits he does not know how to release them or reverse what's been done** — because the **release step (viii "Of the Breaking") was invisible ink on his copy**, deliberately concealed. The **[Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)** just exposed it to him; his gaunt reaction is the realization the escape existed all along and was kept from him.
- **Not malice — zealotry.** He firmly believes he is **"saving them"** from some **"unforeseen future,"** and that certainty overrides the horror of what he's done. The Crusader conviction that justifies anything.
- **Strong candidate for the unwitting ORIGIN of the Hollowing.** Established campaign truth: *the Crusaders errantly created the Hollowing (a corrupted purification rite); the Circle weaponized it.* A well-meaning Inquisitor-Scholar who invented a soul-purification he can't control or reverse is exactly that "errant creator." **He made the lock; Eldrix may have made the key.**
- **Contrast with the party's own discovery:** the **Echo-naming release** at Palewater (speaking a silenced soul's true name FREES it) may be the very "release" mechanism Garrow lacks. Consider holding this card close rather than handing it to him.

## On Mortivar (his account)
- Garrow told the party that **[Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) was "one of the first to explore purification of the soul through magic"** — placing Garrow in the **same research lineage** as the party's main antagonist. Garrow's life's work continues the path Mortivar pioneered.
- Per Garrow: Mortivar **fell from grace**, has been **missing for years**, and the **Crusaders lost his studies.** Garrow believes Mortivar is merely *missing* — **the party knows more** (Ennic/Otto: alive, leading the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)).
- Today's Crusaders **blame Mortivar for the necromantic blight** spreading across the land. Garrow does not seem to see that **his own "humane purification" and Mortivar's "blight" may be the same corrupted rite.**
- **Open table decision:** whether to tell this zealot that Mortivar is likely alive and leading the Circle — could make Garrow an ally, or send a fanatic (with the party's knowledge) straight toward Eldrix.
