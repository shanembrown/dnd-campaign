# Bael Threnfall

## Role
Ruler of the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)

## Location
[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md), [Forden](locations/forden.md)

## Background
Son of **Vael Threnfall** (deceased), the previous ruler who broke the alliance with [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) after the 100 Year War. Bael is the current head of the Threnfall ruling family.

## Connection to the Party
The party has been sent by the Groveborne Rangers to meet Bael and propose reforging the old alliance, carrying the [Broken Silver Coin](items/broken-silver-coin.md) as proof of their mission.
