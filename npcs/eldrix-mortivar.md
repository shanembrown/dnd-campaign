# Eldrix Mortivar

## Status
Active threat - the party's primary antagonist.

## Description
A fallen [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) who has become a dark necromancer. Formerly held the rank of **Inquisitor** within the Crusaders. Was one of their most zealous members before falling from grace, becoming obsessed with both light and dark magic.

## Location
Unknown

## Background
Once a member of the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) — known as **Inquisitor Mortivar** — Eldrix fell from their ranks and turned to necromancy, founding the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md). **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md) suspects (and the party's records confirm) that Eldrix is the Circle's leader** — matching [Xellath](npcs/xellath.md)'s dying reference to "**the one who oversees all.**"

## Known Rituals
- **The Hollowing** — victims disappear for a few days; when found, their eyes have been removed. Mortivar is known for this ritual. The crowned female shrine near the [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple (gouged, bleeding eyes) may be connected.
- **The Hollowing is a corruption of a [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) purification rite** (confirmed at Candlekeep; Hazik identified the obsidian disc as a corrupted Crusader ritual object). The original rite was an **exorcism to cleanse demonic/Abyssal magic from a soul**; inverted, it instead **scoops out the soul/self and leaves an empty vessel**. As a fallen *Inquisitor* obsessed with both light and dark, Eldrix is the natural author of that inversion.
- **200-year history:** the [Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md) records Hollowing cases for two centuries — far longer than Eldrix's lifetime — so he is likely the current **master/systematizer** of an old corruption rather than its inventor.
- New variant observed: [a Hollowed woman](npcs/hollowed-woman.md) imprisoned at [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) has **no eyes AND cannot hear** — suggests the ritual may have evolved, or that an additional layer of magic strips other senses

## Connection to the Party
- The primary antagonist; **suspected leader of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)**
- [Danior's](pcs/danior.md) village was raided long ago — his younger brother **[Draffy](pcs/danior.md)**, animals, and a young girl were all subjected to The Hollowing. Eldrix is the man who hollowed Danior's brother.
- **His seal — a stylized "M" — was found inside the Avowed's secret [Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md) vault** at Candlekeep, on a sealed scroll. Implies a direct line into the order (and possibly to the compromised [Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)).

## Garrow's Account (Palewater)
- **[Inquisitor-Scholar Teodric Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)** — a still-loyal Crusader researching **humane soul-purification** — told the party that **Mortivar was "one of the first to explore purification of the soul through magic."** This places Garrow and Mortivar in the **same research lineage**; Garrow's life's work is a continuation of the very path Mortivar pioneered.
- Per Garrow: Mortivar **fell from grace**, has been **missing for years**, and the **Penance Crusaders lost the information of his studies.** (The party may be holding pieces of that lost knowledge: the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)** and the sealed **"M" scroll** — *M for Mortivar*.)
- **Today's Crusaders believe Mortivar is behind the necromancy/blight spreading across the land** — corroborating the party's intel that Eldrix leads the Circle. Note: Garrow thinks Mortivar is only *missing*; **the party knows more** (Ennic/Otto: alive and leading the Circle).
- **Dramatic irony:** Garrow blames Mortivar for the blight while himself freezing 180 souls in the Palewater salt pans with no way to release them — his "humane cure" and Mortivar's "blight" may be the **same corrupted rite** at different stages.
- **DELIBERATE ERASURE (Palewater).** Shown the **[Rite of the Vessel](items/the-rite-of-the-vessel.md)** (Mortivar's *merciful* original, signed "M"), Garrow challenged its authenticity: **the Penance Crusaders have NO documentation of Mortivar whatsoever.** Combined with the Crusaders having "lost his studies," this reads as a **deliberate scrub**, not a loss. What survives the erasure is telling: the **merciful rite is gone** from Church records, the **corrupted rite (wearing the Penance cross) still circulates**, and the **"Mortivar = the blight" narrative is preserved and taught** — a curated story that makes Mortivar the **scapegoat** while his stolen, inverted rite does the actual killing. Points at the **Circle/Eldrix** or a **compromised Church faction** (cf. the compromised **[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)**).
- **The truth survived only in the Avowed's vault.** The original rite existed only behind a **never-opened, magic-locked shelf at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md)**, reachable via **[Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**'s riddle. The **Avowed preserved what the Crusaders erased** — which makes the document *more* credible, not less.

## Notes
- **Possible cure angle:** if the Hollowing is a corrupted purification rite, the *original, uncorrupted* rite may **reverse** it — making the Crusaders' **old-saints faction** (e.g., [Brother Elrick Vey](npcs/elrick-vey.md)) priceless. The cure and the curse may be the same ritual run in opposite directions.
- **Decapitation strategy:** if Eldrix is the singular head, the Circle may be centralized (and fragile) — but he is correspondingly powerful and guarded.
