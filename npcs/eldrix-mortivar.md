# Eldrix Mortivar

## Status
Active threat - the party's primary antagonist.

## Description
A fallen [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) who has become a dark necromancer. Formerly held the rank of **Inquisitor** within the Crusaders. Was one of their most zealous members before falling from grace, becoming obsessed with both light and dark magic.

## Location
Unknown

## Background
Once a member of the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) — known as **Inquisitor Mortivar** — Eldrix fell from their ranks and turned to necromancy, founding the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md).

## Known Rituals
- **The Hollowing** — victims disappear for a few days; when found, their eyes have been removed. Mortivar is known for this ritual. The crowned female shrine near the [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple (gouged, bleeding eyes) may be connected.

## Connection to the Party
- The primary antagonist
- [Danior's](pcs/danior.md) village was raided long ago — his younger brother, animals, and a young girl were all subjected to The Hollowing
