# Finn and Creel

## Role
Groveborne Rangers — siblings

## Location
Finn: **deceased** — killed near [Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md), buried by the party
Creel: unknown (last seen at [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md))

## Background
Rangers of The Groveborne, called upon by the Keeper to help investigate the cultist telepathy rings recovered from [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md).

## Finn (deceased)
- Covered **Southern Forden**
- Young, newer Groveborne Ranger — bubbly, naive, fun personality; polar opposite of her brother Creel
- Field report: [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) have tripled patrols with daily military drills — clearly preparing for something
- 3 Crusader patrols have failed to return in the last month; official explanation is "banditry" but Finn didn't buy it
- Assigned by the Keeper to escort the party to [Forden](locations/forden.md)
- Had a **secret affair** with **Halden Vayer**, a [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md)
- **Killed** by a wounded Crusader rider near Wicks Hollow — the rider saw her as a traitor due to her affair with Halden and stabbed her in a fit of righteous indignation
- Her wisp network message about [Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md) was **never sent** — the Groveborne does not know about the village, her death, or the Crusader betrayal

## Creel
- Covers **Northern Forden**
- Has a darker background than Finn
- Suspiciously reported "nothing new" despite dark ships being spotted off [Wilden Isle](locations/wilden-isle.md) heading toward his territory
- Voted against the alliance mission ("it's worthless") but was outvoted
- Has a notable rift with [Klif](npcs/klif.md) — party sensed there's more to it (insight check)
- Does not yet know his sister is dead

## Notes
- Met at the Ranger Assembly in the Heart Chamber
- **Halden Vayer** — Penance Crusader, Finn's secret lover. Current status unknown.
