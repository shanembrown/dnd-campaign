# Finn and Creel

## Role
Groveborne Rangers

## Location
[The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md)

## Background
Rangers of The Groveborne, called upon by the Keeper to help investigate the cultist telepathy rings recovered from [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md).

## Finn
- Covers **Southern Forden**
- Field report: [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) have tripled patrols with daily military drills — clearly preparing for something
- 3 Crusader patrols have failed to return in the last month; official explanation is "banditry" but Finn doesn't buy it given Crusader military strength
- Assigned by the Keeper to accompany the party to [Forden](locations/forden.md) (escort role — will not enter the capital)

## Creel
- Covers **Northern Forden**
- Has a darker background than Finn
- Suspiciously reported "nothing new" despite dark ships being spotted off [Wilden Isle](locations/wilden-isle.md) heading toward his territory
- Voted against the alliance mission ("it's worthless") but was outvoted
- Has a notable rift with [Klif](npcs/klif.md) — party sensed there's more to it (insight check)

## Notes
- Met at the Ranger Assembly in the Heart Chamber
