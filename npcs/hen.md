# Hen

## Description
A **potboy** (a young child) at the **inn** in **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)** — the **[grey cat](npcs/the-grey-cat.md)'s favorite person in town.** Silenced with the town; his **Echo** appeared out of a **water reflection** in the inn kitchen.

## Notes
- **Freed by name:** the party said **"Hen"** and the child-Echo was released, saying **"freedom is sweet; but there are more."** — the line that spurred the party to go **building-to-building freeing the rest** of Palewater's Echoes.
- The grey cat pointing the party to Hen specifically reads as the cat **steering them toward mercy.**
- **BACK, and working.** The day after the Breaking, Hen is at the **Salt Croc Inn** as potboy again — one of only two familiar faces to the party in a town otherwise restored to strangers. **He is the party's proof-of-cure in person:** the boy whose Echo they freed by name is alive and himself.
- **Question worth asking him:** does he remember the Echo, the naming, or saying *"freedom is sweet; but there are more"*? If he does, the party has a **witness to the inside of a Hollowing** — priceless for Draffy. If he doesn't, the restoration is clean but blind.
