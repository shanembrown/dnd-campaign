# Eldra Branch

> First name/surname spelling unconfirmed ("Eldra Branch").

## Status
Ally — an [Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md) scholar at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md). Helped the party escape when the Crusaders arrived.

## Description
A scholar of the Avowed, **late 20s**, perpetually **ink-stained** (reads and writes constantly). The **first person at Candlekeep genuinely pleased to see [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md)** — likely an old friend or peer from his time in the order. Warm where the order's leadership was cool.

## Connection to the Party
- Knows [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) personally and welcomed his return
- Familiar with **[Draffy](pcs/danior.md)** ([Danior](pcs/danior.md)'s brother) from the Avowed's **records** — the documented Hollowing case
- **Escorted the party to the library** and the never-opened, magically-locked shelf (the [Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md) vault)
- When **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md)** and the Crusaders arrived hunting the party, **Eldra showed them the back way out** down into the port town
- Vouched to **[Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)** that the party needed passage to **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)** — securing their escape by sea

## Notes
- A reliable **grassroots ally** independent of the compromised Avowed leadership (**[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)**)
- A natural Abyssal-translation resource, though the party now also carries **[The Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)**
- Unclear whether she remained at Candlekeep (exposed to Sevrin/Marrek) or could be a future contact
