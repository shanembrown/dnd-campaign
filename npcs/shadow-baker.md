# The Shadow Baker

## Status
Encountered by the party in [The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md).

## Description
A mysterious vendor found in the deeper reaches of [The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md). Sells pastries and cakes, each with a unique magical or whimsical effect.

## Location
[The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md)

## Wares
Magical baked goods with unique whimsical outcomes.

| Item | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Donut | Transforms the eater into a frog |
| Orange Gillette | Unknown (purchased by Hazik) |

## Connection to the Party
Re-encountered by the party during their search for [Calef](npcs/calef.md) in the deeper parts of The Rootpath.
