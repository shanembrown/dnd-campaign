# Xellath

## Status
Dead — killed himself during interrogation

## Description
A powerful cultist spellcaster associated with the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md). Capable of casting Hold Person and summoning Wights.

## Location
[Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple

## Background
Fought alongside Zaelar at the Wilden Reach Temple in the hardest battle the party has faced. Knocked unconscious and captured alive by the party.

## Interrogation
- When asked who his leader was, referenced "the one who oversees all" — spoke of this figure with reverence as all-powerful, more as a zealot's idol than a direct boss
- Confirmed the name [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) in this context
- When asked where he came from, incanted a spell and killed himself rather than reveal the information
- Had an Abyssal inscription on him — the party recovered it but [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) later destroyed it
- Wore a telepathy ring used for cultist communication

## Notes
- Captured after a brutal fight — multiple party members went down
- Party planned to interrogate using [Medallion of Thoughts](items/medallion-of-thoughts.md) but Xellath killed himself before extensive questioning
