# Hazik

## Class
Wizard

## Level
4

## Background
**Hazik is one of [The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md)** — raised and trained at [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md). He **left the order over a year ago** to *apply* magic in the world, which puts him at odds with the Avowed philosophy of gathering knowledge but not using it. His uncle and father-figure, **[Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**, raised him.

## Notes
- Returning to Candlekeep, Hazik was **welcomed "home"** by [Magister Korinvelle](npcs/magister-korinvelle.md) (coolly) and warmly by his old peer **[Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md)**
- Visited his **dying uncle [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**, who gave him **[The Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)** (heirloom; translates any text), the warning about [Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)'s compromise, the lead on [Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md), and the cryptic phrase "eight bone pieces; the chain circles around"
- Ennic was **saddened** that Hazik has taken on the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) as his "cross to bear"
- The party's arcane brain and Avowed insider; identified the obsidian disc (corrupted Crusader rite), the bone whistle, and [Vord's brass key](items/avowed-brass-keys.md) for [Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)
