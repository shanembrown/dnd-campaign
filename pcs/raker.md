## Background
Criminal — orphaned young, Raker turned to a life of petty crime out of necessity rather than malice. Despite looking like a child (halfling), he's actually fairly old. As he aged he left that life behind, seeking simplicity and eventually finding his path as a Druid. He finds solace in nature and is driven by doing right by the world.

### Step 2 — Ability Score Improvement (ASI) vs Feat
**Decision pending.** Options discussed:

| Option | What it gives | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **+2 WIS (15→17)** | Mod +2→+3; Spell DC 13→14; Shillelagh +1; 1 more prepared spell | Simpler play, stronger control spells |
| **+2 CON (14→16)** | Mod +2→+3; +1 HP per level retroactive (+4 now); better concentration saves | "Tank" durability |
| **War Caster (feat)** | Advantage on concentration saves; cast spells with hands full; cast as opportunity attack | Protecting battlefield-control spells |

**Current plan:** War Caster if it loads in Foundry (DM is enabling more content). Fallback: **+2 WIS** (clean, immediate spell DC boost).

**How to pick War Caster in Foundry:**
1. On the ASI screen, **uncheck** the "Ability Score Improvement Feat" box (which is the default stat-bump)
2. Click "Select Feat" — Compendium Browser opens
3. Filter: **FEATURE TYPE → General Feat**
4. Find **War Caster**, check it, hit SELECT
5. If War Caster isn't there, the DM needs to load more content (it's not in default SRD compendium)

**How to pick +2 WIS in Foundry:**
1. Leave the "Ability Score Improvement Feat" box checked
2. Click the `+` next to Wisdom **twice** (15 → 17)
3. Confirm 0 points remaining

### Step 3 — New Cantrip
Druid knows 2 cantrips at level 1, **3 at level 4**.

**Recommended: Thorn Whip**
- 1d6 piercing, 30-ft range
- Pulls the target **10 ft toward you** on a hit
- Combos with Spike Growth (drag enemies through spikes for huge damage), and gives Raker a ranged option

Other strong picks if you want to reconsider:
- **Produce Flame** — clean ranged 1d8 fire attack, doubles as a torch
- **Mold Earth** — utility/roleplay (shape stone, create cover)
- **Guidance** — +1d4 to one ally's ability check (out of combat utility)

### Step 4 — Spell Updates
Druids prepare from the full druid list each long rest, so this isn't "learn a new spell" — it's "you now prepare more."

- Prepared spells = Druid level + Wis mod
- With +2 WIS: 4 + 3 = **7 prepared spells**
- With War Caster (no Wis bump): 4 + 2 = **6 prepared spells**

Suggested daily prep heading into Forden/Candlekeep:
- **Entangle** (proven powerful in last session)
- **Faerie Fire** (Dex-save alternative)
- **Healing Word** (bonus-action heal — saves downed allies)
- **Goodberry** (out-of-combat utility, food + 1 HP each)
- **Spike Growth** (2nd level — battlefield control)
- **Pass Without Trace** (2nd level — **+10 stealth for the whole party**; cracked for sneaking around Crusader territory)
- One flex slot (Cure Wounds, Moonbeam, or another situational pick)
