# Danior

## Class
Cleric

## Level
4

## Background
Danior's village was raided long ago. His younger brother — named **Draffy** *(spelling TBC)* — along with a number of animals and a young girl, all disappeared for a few days. When they were found, their eyes had been removed. This is now understood to be **The Hollowing**, a ritual [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) is known for — now understood to be a **corruption of a [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) purification rite**.

## Draffy (brother)
- **[The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md) have records of Draffy** — [Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md) knew of him from the order's documented Hollowing case
- A **personal stake** for Danior: the man who hollowed his brother (Eldrix) is the founder/leader of the Circle, and the **cure may be the Crusaders' original purification rite**
- Open question to mine from the [Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md): **was Draffy's case ever reversed, and is he recoverable?**
