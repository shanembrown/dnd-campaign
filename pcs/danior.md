# Danior

## Class
Cleric

## Level
3

## Background
Danior's village was raided long ago. His younger brother — along with a number of animals and a young girl — all disappeared for a few days. When they returned or were found, their eyes had been removed. This is now understood to be a ritual known as **The Hollowing**, which [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) is known for.
