# The Avowed

> Name spelling unconfirmed ("Avowed").

## Description
The **scholars who run [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md)** on [Forden](locations/forden.md) — a famous library fortress city. The Avowed are **scholars, not soldiers**; Candlekeep is *not* a Penance Crusader holding. They occupy the monastery/keep on the hill above the port town. [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md) confirmed their presence.

## Connection to the Party
- **[Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) is one of the Avowed** — raised and trained at Candlekeep; left the order over a year ago to apply magic in the world. The order **gathers knowledge but does not use it**, so Hazik's path is at odds with their philosophy.
- The party briefed the Avowed on the necromancy/rituals they've witnessed; the Avowed **know of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)** and hold a **well-documented case of the Hollowing** (the 200-year **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)**)

## Key Figures
- **[Magister Korinvelle](npcs/magister-korinvelle.md)** — senior Avowed; welcomed Hazik home (coolly)
- **[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)** — the order's **leader**, now **COMPROMISED** (feeding all Avowed information to the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md))
- **[Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md)** — scholar, warm to Hazik; grassroots ally
- **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)** — "tinkerer of knowledge," the most knowledgeable figure met
- **[Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)** — Hazik's dying uncle/father-figure
- **[Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)** — a former Brother, now in [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)

## Notes
- **Scholars, not soldiers** — a knowledge order, not a military one
- Run Candlekeep: the port town (commonfolk + Avowed) and the monastery/keep on the hill
- **Historically held allegiance to no one** — but their leader Sevrin has broken that neutrality, aligning with the Crusaders. **Trust the grassroots (Eldra, Otto, Ennic), not the throne.**
- Candlekeep has **changed since Hazik left** (1+ year ago): **doors locked, more strange creatures, people on edge** — the order is frightened and fraying
