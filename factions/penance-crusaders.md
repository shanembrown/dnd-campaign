# Penance Crusaders

## Overview
A faction of crusaders who fight against dark magic.

## Goals


## Notable Members
- **[Bael Threnfall](npcs/bael-threnfall.md)** — current ruler (son of Vael)
- **Vael Threnfall** (deceased) — former ruler, broke the alliance coin with [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) after the 100 Year War
- [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) (former) — one of the most zealous Crusaders; fell from grace and became obsessed with both light and dark magic, founding the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)

## Alliances
- Formerly allied with [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) to cleanse the dark magic of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md); alliance broken after the 100 Year War (symbolized by a [broken silver coin](items/broken-silver-coin.md))
- [Order of the Sentinel Guards](factions/order-of-the-sentinel-guards.md) serves as their military arm

## Known Locations
- **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)** — capital city on [Forden](locations/forden.md), seat of the Threnfall ruling family
- [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) — only stronghold in [Wilden](locations/the-groveborne.md)

## History
- Formed an alliance with The Groveborne and together cleansed the dark magic of the Circle of the Occult
- Alliance was broken after the **100 Year War** — Keeper Caelwyn and Vael Threnfall broke a silver coin to symbolize the split
- The Circle of the Occult has close ties to the Crusaders, as its founder (Mortivar) was one of their most zealous members

## Current Status
- [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) believes the Crusaders may have become too zealous and their vision "clouded"
- They were once friends of The Groveborne but the relationship is strained
- Despite concerns, they likely understand Abyssal better than anyone, having spent decades studying it
- Tripled patrols and daily military drills — clearly preparing for something
- 3 Crusader patrols have failed to return; official word is "banditry" but this seems implausible
- The party has been sent by the Groveborne Rangers to propose reforging the alliance, carrying the [Broken Silver Coin](items/broken-silver-coin.md) to [Bael Threnfall](npcs/bael-threnfall.md)
- **Doctrinal schism**: the modern order has drifted from "the older saints"; followers of the old tradition are now branded heretics and imprisoned (e.g., [Brother Elrick Vey](npcs/elrick-vey.md) at Crusader Keep). May explain the betrayed rider at Wicks Hollow and [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md)'s assassination
