# Penance Crusaders

## Overview
A faction of crusaders who fight against dark magic.

## Goals


## Notable Members
- [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) (former) - fell from the order and became a dark necromancer

## Alliances
- Allied with [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) to cleanse the dark magic of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)
- [Order of the Sentinel Guards](factions/order-of-the-sentinel-guards.md) serves as their military arm

## Known Locations
- Capital on [Forden](locations/forden.md)
- [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) — only stronghold in [Wilden](locations/the-groveborne.md)

## History
Formed an alliance with The Groveborne and together cleansed the dark magic of the Circle of the Occult. However, this dark magic is now resurfacing.

## Current Status
- [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) believes the Crusaders may have become too zealous and their vision "clouded"
- They were once friends of The Groveborne but the relationship is strained
- Despite concerns, they likely understand Abyssal better than anyone, having spent decades studying it
- The party may attempt to mend the rift between the Groveborne and the Crusaders as neutral "outsiders"
