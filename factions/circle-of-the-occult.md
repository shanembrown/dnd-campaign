# Circle of the Occult

## Overview
Enemies of [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md). Practitioners of dark magic, including necromancy.

## Goals


## Symbols
- [Sigil of the Occult](items/sigil-of-the-occult.md) - markings found on members and affiliated bandits

## Members
- Bandits along the Fangorne Mountains ridgeline (defeated by the party)
- [Morwen](npcs/morwen.md) - hag in the [Stagnant Swamp](locations/stagnant-swamp.md) (defeated)
- **Xellath** — powerful cultist spellcaster at [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple, capable of Hold Person and summoning Wights (captured alive)
- **Zaelar** — named cultist at Wilden Reach Temple (killed)

## Known Locations
- [Fangorne Mountain Overlook](locations/fangorne-mountain-overlook.md) - camp encountered by the party
- [Featherthorne Hollow](locations/featherthorne-hollow.md) - cave where necromancy was encountered
- [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple — cultist outpost with summoning rituals, similar magic to Featherthorne Hollow

## History
- Attacked The Groveborne but were fought off by [Sage Nightbloom](npcs/sage-nightbloom.md) and others
- Their dark magic was cleansed by an alliance between [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) and the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md), but is now resurfacing
- **Close ties to the Penance Crusaders** — founded by [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md), who was one of the most zealous Crusaders before falling from grace and becoming obsessed with both light and dark magic. **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md) suspects Eldrix also *leads* the Circle** ("the one who oversees all").
- **The Hollowing is their signature weapon** — a **corruption of the Crusaders' purification rite** (see [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)); 200 years of cases recorded in the [Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)

## Communication network
- Coordinates via **paired/networked signaling**: the [Telepathy Rings](items/telepathy-rings.md) (thought; needs 8, party has 2) and a **bone whistle** (a paired locator — playing one tells its counterpart your location)
- [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)'s riddle — **"eight bone pieces; the chain circles around"** — may describe this network (a closed chain of eight) and hint at a vulnerability
