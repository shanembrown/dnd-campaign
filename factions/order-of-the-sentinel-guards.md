# Order of the Sentinel Guards

## Description
Keepers of the kingdom. Effectively the military arm of the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md). An order that bestows the title of "Lightkeeper" upon its members.

## Known Members
- [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) - Lightkeeper, based in the south

## Connections
- Historic ties to [The Groveborn](locations/the-groveborn.md) — Istren Vale's grandfather was a family friend of [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md)
