# Southreach

## Description
A small coastal fishing village at the southern tip of Wilden, below the [Skyreach Mountains](locations/skyreach-mountains.md). Part of [Southern Wilden](locations/southern-wilden.md). Huts with grass roofs.

## Features
- Small village, not many residents
- Grass-roofed huts
- Coastal fishing community

## Encounters
- Party met [Lightkeeper Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) of the [Order of the Sentinel Guards](factions/order-of-the-sentinel-guards.md) here

## Significance
- Home of [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md)
- [Reif](npcs/reif.md) suggested the party investigate [Southern Wilden](locations/southern-wilden.md) — Southreach is their first stop

## Notes
[Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) retreated here after his party encountered overwhelming forces in the [Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md) — he was the only survivor.
