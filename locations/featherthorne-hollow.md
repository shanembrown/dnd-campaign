# Featherthorne Hollow

## Description
A location containing a cave where the party encountered dark magic.

## Features
- A cave with signs of necromancy

## Encounters
- The party encountered necromancy here, evidence that the dark magic of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) is resurfacing

## Significance
First sign that the dark magic thought to be cleansed by the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) and [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) alliance has returned.

## Notes
The same demonic magic was later found being performed by [Morwen](npcs/morwen.md) the Hag in the [Stagnant Swamp](locations/stagnant-swamp.md), suggesting a wider pattern of resurgence.
