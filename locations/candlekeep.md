# Candlekeep

## Description
A **famous library fortress city** on the **continent of [Forden](locations/forden.md)** — a renowned center of knowledge, **run by [The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md)** (scholars, **not** soldiers). **It is NOT a [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) fortress.** Party is sailing here aboard [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)'s ship, **[The Thornbill](locations/the-thornbill.md)**, after escaping the Wilden [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md).

## Layout
- A **port town right off the docks** — home to **commonfolk and the Avowed**
- The **monastery / keep up on the hill** — the Avowed's library fortress proper

## Notes
- A **famous library fortress city run by the Avowed** — its renown as a seat of knowledge makes it a strong bet for **Abyssal** research and rare lore
- **Run by scholars, not soldiers** — far friendlier ground than Crusader territory. The party are not fugitives here, and the Avowed (Hazik's connection) are the local authority
- **Tone contrast:** the Wilden [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) is **militaristic**; Candlekeep is more a **magistrate / cleric city** — governance, scholarship, and faith rather than swords
- First mentioned as the destination after the escape — unclear whether it was the party's choice, Captain Merrin's preferred port, or a stop on the way to [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)
- Town size, character, governance, and disposition toward the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) all unknown — needs in-session discovery
- Worth investigating on arrival: any library, scribes, or scholars who might help with **Abyssal** (the party's open need to read the [Black Iron Nail](items/black-iron-nail.md) and related inscriptions)
- **[The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md) have a presence here** (confirmed by [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)), and **[Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) has a connection to them** — a ready-made contact on arrival
- **[Onna Kelp](npcs/onna-kelp.md) is quite familiar with Candlekeep** — a useful local guide when the party lands
