# Candlekeep

## Description
A town on the **continent of [Forden](locations/forden.md)**. Party is sailing here aboard [Captain Marin](npcs/captain-marin.md)'s ship after escaping [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md).

## Notes
- First mentioned as the destination after the escape — unclear whether it was the party's choice, Captain Marin's preferred port, or a stop on the way to [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)
- Town size, character, governance, and disposition toward the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) all unknown — needs in-session discovery
- Worth investigating on arrival: any library, scribes, or scholars who might help with **Abyssal** (the party's open need to read the [Black Iron Nail](items/black-iron-nail.md) and related inscriptions)
