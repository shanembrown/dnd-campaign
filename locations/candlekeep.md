# Candlekeep

## Description
A **famous library fortress city** on the **continent of [Forden](locations/forden.md)** — a renowned center of knowledge, **run by [The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md)** (scholars, **not** soldiers). **It is NOT a [Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md) fortress.** Party is sailing here aboard [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)'s ship, **[The Thornbill](locations/the-thornbill.md)**, after escaping the Wilden [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md).

## Layout
- A **port town right off the docks** — home to **commonfolk and the Avowed**
- The **monastery / keep up on the hill** — the Avowed's library fortress proper

## What happened here (the party landed)
The party arrived at Candlekeep after the ~2-week voyage. **[Onna Kelp](npcs/onna-kelp.md) gave them a map.** They visited **[The Reaver's Tooth](locations/the-reavers-tooth.md)** (a port bar) before heading up to the Avowed.

- **Crusader omnipresence:** despite being an Avowed (scholar) city, the **[Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)** now have a heavy presence around Candlekeep. Doors are locked, strange creatures are appearing, people are on edge — the city has **changed since [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) left** (1+ year ago).
- **Hazik welcomed "home":** Hazik is revealed to be **one of the Avowed**. [Magister Korinvelle](npcs/magister-korinvelle.md) welcomed him coolly; **[Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md)** warmly.
- **Major reveals:** the **Hollowing is a corrupted Crusader purification rite**; the Avowed know of the **Circle of the Occult** and **[Draffy](pcs/danior.md)** (Danior's brother); **[Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)** suspected as the Circle's leader.
- **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)** (basement tinkerer) identified three items in exchange for help with the party's Abyssal document.
- **[Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)** (Hazik's dying uncle) gave Hazik **[The Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)**, warned that **[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)** (the Avowed's leader) is **compromised**, and sent the party to find **[Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)** in [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md).
- **The library vault:** Eldra led the party to a never-opened, magic-locked shelf (solved with Ennic's riddle), recovering the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)** (200 years of cases), a scroll sealed with **Eldrix's "M" mark**, and an **unmarked brass key**.
- **Escape:** **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md)** arrived hunting the party (Sevrin's tip-off). Eldra showed them the back way out down to the port; they ran into **[Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)** and sailed for **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)** (Merrin's heading anyway).

## Layout (confirmed)
- A **port town off the docks** (commonfolk + Avowed), with the **monastery/library fortress up on the hill**
- **[The Reaver's Tooth](locations/the-reavers-tooth.md)** — a port bar

## Notes
- The Avowed have **historically held allegiance to no one** — but leader Sevrin's compromise means the party are **not safe here** despite it being a "scholar" city
