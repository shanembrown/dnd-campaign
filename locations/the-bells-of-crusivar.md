# The Bells of Crusivar

The great **cathedral belltower** at the heart of **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)** rings **on the hour — twelve bells at once.** When they ring, **the whole city stops and falls silent**, everyone meditating in place. It is universal, hourly, and observed by all.

**It is also the party's best lead in the capital.**

## THE EAST TOWER BELL IS OFF
One bell — **the east tower** — is **very slightly off.** Off time, off the beat with the other eleven.

- **The party noticed.**
- **So did other people.** Several others in the city have independently indicated they've noticed it too. This is not paranoia; it is a real, observable irregularity that the city is quietly aware of.

## WHY THAT MATTERS — the bell-ringer is COUNTING
The party observed the **Crusader ringing the east bell during the day**, and he is **counting.**

Every other ringer rings **purely by feel.**

**Confirmed with sources:** ringing is **supposed** to be done by feel — the rhythm is **ingrained** in the ringers through training. **Bell-ringer is a high, sought-after rank** in the Crusivar hierarchy. These are not casual appointments; they are elite, trained, and the training is precisely what makes counting unnecessary.

**So the man in the east tower is either:**
1. **Not who he is supposed to be** — an outsider who never received the training and is faking a rank he doesn't hold, or
2. **Not doing what the others are doing** — deliberately ringing to a *different* count. Not devotion. **Signal.**

A slightly-off bell, rung on a deliberate count, over a city that stops dead every hour, is **a message being broadcast to everyone who knows to listen.**

## THE PEOPLE WHO DON'T STOP
Splitting up to observe, **[Raker](pcs/raker.md) and [Danior](pcs/danior.md)** followed a member of the crowd and found:

**There are people among the crowd who are IN SYNC with each other — but who do NOT stop to meditate during the bells like the rest of the city.**

This is the single most important observation of the night:

- **They are synchronised.** *"In sync"* is the language of **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)**, where ~180 entranced townsfolk **breathed and pulsed as one**, and of the **Hollowing** itself.
- **They are exempt from the ritual.** Everyone else stops. They don't. Either they are **not bound by whatever the bells do**, or they are **working during the silence** — the one moment every hour when the entire city has its eyes closed.
- **They move among the crowd.** They are not marked, robed, or set apart. They pass as ordinary citizens.

**The obvious pairing:** an off-count bell in the east tower and a synchronised group that ignores the ritual. **The bell may be their clock.**

## Open Questions
- **Who is the east-tower ringer?** Is he a Crusader at all? How did an outsider take a sought-after rank?
- **Is the off-beat a signal, a countdown, or a flaw?** If it's a count, *what number*, and does it change?
- **What do the synced ones do during the silence?**
- **Does the ash mark relate?** Everyone wears one; do the synced ones?
- **Is the hourly silence itself a rite?** A city-wide, daily, compulsory stillness in the capital of the order whose purification rite became the Hollowing.
- **Eight** is the Circle's number (Ennic's riddle, the 8-tile lock, the 8-ring altar, 8 figures at the Waystation). **Twelve** is the bells. **Twelve** was also **[Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)'s "first 12"** at the centre of Palewater — the binding keystone that made 180 souls breathe as one.
