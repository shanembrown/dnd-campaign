# Wicks Hollow

## Description
A small village in southeastern [Wilden](locations/the-groveborne.md), along the route to [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) and the coast.

## Current State
**Abandoned** — the party found the village completely empty. Food still on plates (being eaten by animals), fires still lit. The abandonment was abrupt and recent (~2 days prior). Survival check (nat 20, Danior) confirmed the timeline.

Animals communicated (via Speak with Animals) that all the humans got up and left **at the exact same time**.

## Significance
- Suspected to be the work of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md), possibly using [The Hollowing](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md) to compel villagers en masse
- Supports the theory that the Circle is raising an army through The Hollowing — mass mind control to harvest people
- [Finn](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) was killed here by a wounded Crusader rider before she could relay this intelligence to the Groveborne via the wisp network
- The Groveborne does **not** know about this village's fate

## Encounters
- Party camped off the road near the village overnight
- A wounded, armored Crusader rider arrived on horseback — gravely injured, fleeing betrayal within the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)
- Danior stabilized the rider with Healing Word and used the [Medallion of Thoughts](items/medallion-of-thoughts.md) to read his mind
- Learned: [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) is dead, betrayed alongside this rider by fellow Crusaders; the rider was heading to [Keeper Caelwyn](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) for help
- The rider stabbed and killed [Finn](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) upon learning of her secret affair with Halden Vayer (a Crusader) — saw her as a traitor

## Notes
- Party buried [Finn](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) and the Crusader rider near Wicks Hollow before continuing east toward [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md)
