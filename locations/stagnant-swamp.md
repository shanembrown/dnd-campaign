# Stagnant Swamp

## Description
A murky, boggy swamp south of [The Groveborn](locations/the-groveborn.md). The ground is wet and unstable, and the air carries the smell of stale, rotting bog. The forest thins as the terrain transitions from dense woodland into the swamp.

## Features


## Encounters
- Party discovered a hut/cabin containing [Morwen](npcs/morwen.md) the Hag and her cat [Mister Scratch](npcs/mister-scratch.md); Morwen attacked and was defeated. She was performing a ritual using the same demonic magic seen at [Featherthorne Hollow](locations/featherthorne-hollow.md). The ritual liquid is used to turn the dead into undead.
- Party mixed 1/2 purple and green from [Morwen's Recipe Book](items/morwen-recipe-book.md) liquids, creating a grey ooze that attacked them.


## Significance
Lies on the route south toward [Southern Wilden](locations/southern-wilden.md). [Calef](npcs/calef.md) warned that the swamp would be more difficult than the mountains.

## Notes
Party took a long rest at Morwen's hut before continuing south.
