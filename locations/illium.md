# Illium

## Description
The world — an archipelago of islands separated by open sea.

## Known Islands
- [Wilden](locations/the-groveborn.md) - large western island, home of The Groveborn
- [Forden](locations/forden.md) - large eastern island
- [Wilden Isle](locations/wilden-isle.md) - small island off the northeast coast of Wilden
- [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) - small cluster of islands to the southwest of Wilden
- [The Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md) - frozen island to the south
