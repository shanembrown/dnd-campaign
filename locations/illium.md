# Illium

## Description
The world — an archipelago of islands separated by open sea.

## Geography
From the world map:
- **[Wilden](locations/the-groveborne.md)** — large western island, home of The Groveborne. Irregular coastline with a bay on the eastern side
- **[Forden](locations/forden.md)** — large crescent/horseshoe-shaped island to the east. Home of the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) capital
- **[Wilden Isle](locations/wilden-isle.md)** — small island just north of Wilden
- **[Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md)** — small cluster of islands to the southwest of Wilden
- **[The Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md)** — island to the south, between Wilden and Forden
- Two small unnamed islands sit in the sea between Wilden and Forden

## Notes
- Ships are seen traveling the waters between the islands
