## Notable Features
- A great tree at the center
- [The Rootpath](locations/the-rootpath.md) - beneath the central tree

## NPCs
- [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) - leader of The Groveborne
- [Sage Nightbloom](npcs/sage-nightbloom.md) - botanist and defender of The Groveborne
- [Finn and Creel](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) - Groveborne Rangers (summoned by the Keeper, arriving soon)

## History
Attacked by the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md), but the assault was repelled with the help of Sage Nightbloom. Formed an alliance with the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) to cleanse the Circle's dark magic.


## Encounters
- Party restocked provisions (food and clothing) here before departing south
- Party returned after events at [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) to consult with the Keeper
- Relayed intelligence about the cultists, the telepathy rings, the altar, and [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md)'s suspicious behavior
- Keeper confirmed the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) may be too zealous but acknowledged they understand Abyssal better than anyone
- Discussed possibility of the party mediating between The Groveborne and the Penance Crusaders as "outsiders"
- Keeper summoned [Finn and Creel](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) to investigate the telepathy rings
