# Fangorne Mountain Overlook

## Description
A mountain overlook along the Fangorne Mountains ridgeline, above The Lost Scar. The path descends from here into the vast valley below.

## Features
- A road along the ridge
- Burned forest along the descent

## Encounters
### Circle of the Occult Camp (defeated)
- Smoke from their campfire was visible from the ridgeline
- Members bore the [Sigil of the Occult](items/sigil-of-the-occult.md), linking them to the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)

## Significance
Last known location where [Calef](npcs/calef.md) saw the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md).
