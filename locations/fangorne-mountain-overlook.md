# Fangorne Mountain Overlook

## Description
A mountain overlook above The Lost Scar. The path descends from here into the vast valley below.

## Features
- A road along the ridge
- Burned forest along the descent

## Encounters
### Bandit Camp (defeated)
- Smoke from their campfire was visible from the ridgeline
- Bandits bore the [Sigil of the Occulet](items/sigil-of-the-occulet.md), linking them to the [Cult of the Occulet](factions/cult-of-the-occulet.md)

## Significance
Last known location where [Klif](npcs/klif.md) saw the [Cult of the Occulet](factions/cult-of-the-occulet.md).
