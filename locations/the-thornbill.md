# The Thornbill

## Description
A small **cog** captained by [Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md). Single mast, square sail, modest rig. **Five crew plus the captain.** The party hired her at the [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md) Docks for the ~2-week voyage to [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md) on [Forden](locations/forden.md).

## Crew
- **[Captain Merrin](npcs/captain-merrin.md)** — owner/captain; takes the helm
- **[Onna Kelp](npcs/onna-kelp.md)** — first mate; trades off at the wheel; drew the ship's cutaway map (signed "O.K.")
- **[Old Brusk](npcs/old-brusk.md)** — cook; the galley is his domain
- **Hask** — crew (quartered in crew quarters)
- Plus other deckhands to make five crew total

## Passenger
- **[Sehir Vondrel](npcs/sehir-vondrel.md)** — lives below decks in the steerage among the cargo; "the crew leaves her be"

## Layout (per Onna Kelp's cutaway sketch)
- **Party Bunks** — under the foredeck; cramped, 4 hammocks, a sea chest, low ceiling. *Only Raker (a halfling) can stand upright.*
- **Helm & Tiller** — Merrin or Onna at the wheel
- **Merrin's Cabin** — stern; window, charts, books. Where serious conversations (and the party's dinner) happen
- **Stores** — spare canvas, water cask
- **The Hold** — salt, wool, ballast; damp, low headroom, the floor creaks
- **Galley** — Old Brusk's domain; smell of pitch and smoke
- **Steerage (Sehir Vondrel's space)** — among the cargo; pallet of straw, a grey cat, one lamp, a small wooden box on a barrel beside her
- **Crew Quarters** — Onna, Hask, and others; hammocks and one dim lantern
- **Below decks generally** — dark, poorly lit (relevant for stealth/scouting)

## Notes
- The crew have remarked they've shipped **far more supplies than usual** (weapons, fortifications) to Crusader Keep lately — see [Crusader Keep](locations/crusader-keep.md)
