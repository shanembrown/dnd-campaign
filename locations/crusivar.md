# Crusivar

## Description
Capital city of the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md), located on [Forden](locations/forden.md).

## Features
- Seat of the **Threnfall** ruling family
- Current ruler: [Bael Threnfall](npcs/bael-threnfall.md) (son of the late Vael Threnfall)

## Significance
The party has been sent here by the Groveborne Rangers to propose reforging the old alliance between [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) and the Penance Crusaders.

## ✅ THE PARTY IS INSIDE THE CITY
The party **long rested at the inn** in the town a day back on the **Drover's Road**, then travelled the final stretch and **entered Crusivar undetected.**

- **The gate was busy** — *a large number of people coming and going.* The crowd did the work the disguise was meant to do.
- **The cover held.** Despite having "made a name" for themselves fighting off the Circle ambush at **[The Waystation](locations/the-waystation.md)**, the party **went unnoticed** and slipped in.
- **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md) is hunting them** and did not catch them at the gate. That does not mean he doesn't know they're coming.

### On waking at the inn
The party woke to **a large amount of animal noise** — unremarkable on the **Drover's Road** (a livestock road), but **unexamined**. Raker has **Speak with Animals** available as a free ritual. *Follow-up: was that just drovers' herds, or was something moving?*

## First Impressions — The Scale of the Place
**Larger than anything the party has ever seen.** This is a real capital, and it reads like one.

- **Very tall gates.**
- **A large cathedral with a belltower.** The bells ring **on the hour — twelve of them, all at once** — and **when they ring, the whole city falls silent.** Everyone stops. This is **not just timekeeping; it is observed, city-wide, as a ritual.**
- **Enormous population and enormous diversity of life.** So much so that **[Raker](pcs/raker.md) and [Danior](pcs/danior.md) do not stand out** — the thing the party feared most about Forden (being non-human, being the wrong size) **is not a problem here.** The crowd is the disguise.

### 🔥 THE ASH MARK
**Every single person in the city bears an imprint of ash on their forehead** — like a thumbprint.

- The party had **no mark**, and **[Jory Tinwhistle](npcs/jory-tinwhistle.md) spotted it immediately** as the thing that would give them away. **They marked themselves** and blended in.
- **Jory knew to look for it.** She has been here before, or knows the custom. Worth noting.
- **Ash = penance.** This is the **[Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)**' capital; a universal ash mark is a visible, daily, compulsory act of contrition — and a **perfect compliance test**. A person without one is instantly visible.

**Open questions about the mark:**
- **Who applies it, and when?** If it's given at the cathedral daily, a self-applied mark may be the *wrong shape*, in the *wrong place*, or *stale by the wrong number of hours*. The party faked a mark without knowing the rite behind it.
- **Does it mean anything else?** Does it vary by rank, ward, or status?
- **Does it do anything?** The party has seen ritual objects that mark and bind people. An ash mark on every forehead in a city run by the people who invented the Hollowing deserves a hard look — **[Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) should check it for magic.**

### ⚠️ THE BELLS AND THE SILENCE
**→ This became the party's main investigation. See [The Bells of Crusivar](locations/the-bells-of-crusivar.md) for the full findings: the off-beat east tower bell, the ringer who COUNTS, and the synchronised people in the crowd who do not stop for the silence.**

**Twelve bells, and the city goes silent.**

**"Silent" is the exact word for what happened to [Palewater](locations/palewater.md).** A whole town, stopped, at the same instant, as one. The party has already seen a mass rite that made ~180 people **breathe and pulse as one.**

A city-wide, hourly, universally observed moment of stillness, in the capital of the order whose purification rite *became* the Hollowing, is either:
1. ordinary devotional practice that the Hollowing was designed to imitate, or
2. **something the party should be very interested in.**

*To test: what happens if someone does NOT stop when the bells ring? Does anyone check? What do people do during the silence — pray, bow, face the cathedral?*

## The Market Street — Walking In
Between the gate and **[The Warm Ash](locations/the-warm-ash.md)**, the party crossed a dense, lantern-lit market.

- **[Odd Tom](npcs/odd-tom.md)**, a **pie vendor** working a cart called **The Ember Pie** — **[Danior](pcs/danior.md)** stopped to buy pies. Brief, friendly, no business done. **A vendor who stands in one spot all day is a good source to come back to.**
- **Stalls selling "odd relics"** — **apparently religious in nature**, and noticed by the party but **not yet examined**. Goods on offer include **pyre-charms, relic bones, ikons, and "salt of ashes."** One stall's sign reads: ***"FOR A CLEANER TOMORROW."***

### ⚠️ Why the relic stalls matter
The market is selling the **vocabulary of everything the party has been chasing**, as souvenirs:

- **"Salt of ashes"** — **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)** was a **salt** town, buried under a thin even layer of salt, with **180 souls standing in salt pans.**
- **"For a cleaner tomorrow"** — *cleansing / purification* is the exact language of the **[Rite of Purification](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)**.
- **Pyre-charms** — **[Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)** spent his life seeking a humane replacement for **the pyre**. **Pyre smoke is visible over the city's rooftops right now.**
- **Relic bones** — **[Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**'s riddle: ***"eight bone pieces; the chain circles around."***

**Unexamined.** Worth a closer look, a **Religion check**, and **[Hazik](pcs/hazik.md)** checking whether any of it is actually magical — these could be devotional trinkets, or debased copies of real ritual objects sold openly because nobody left alive knows what they were for.
