# The Reaver's Tooth

> Name spelling unconfirmed ("The Reaver's Tooth").

## Description
A **bar/tavern in the port town of [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md)**, visited by the party shortly after they first landed (before heading up to the Avowed headquarters).

## Notes
- **The grey cat** seen here later turned up **sleeping on [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)'s chest** at his deathbed. The recurring cat's meaning is **unknown** — likely a deliberate motif (a tavern cat wandering to a dying man's bedside), **not** assumed to be [Sehir Vondrel](npcs/sehir-vondrel.md)'s familiar. Watch for a third appearance.
