# The Lost Scar

## Description
A vast valley with many small paths that branch off along the ridges. Higher elevations along the ridges lead into snow. The scale of this place is immense - days of travel barely scratch the surface of its expanse.

## Features
- Accessible via descent from [Fangorne Mountain Overlook](locations/fangorne-mountain-overlook.md)

## Encounters
- The party traversed the valley, a grueling journey that depleted all their food supplies
- Return journey (from Hightower to The Grove) was uneventful; rations held, no encounters

## Significance
Where [Reif](npcs/reif.md) was located, at [Hightower](locations/hightower.md).

## Recent Intelligence
- A **strange new stone shrine** has been built — not Groveborne-made
- Arranged in a spiral formation, centered on darkened charred black earth that has never been seen before
- [Reif](npcs/reif.md) reports a sense of genuine dread near the shrine and refuses to approach it
- Likely connected to the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)
