# Palewater

## Description
A **salt-harvesting commerce town** on the coast of **[Forden](locations/forden.md)**, reached by sea on the voyage toward **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)**. The community makes its living **collecting salt from the ocean** via shallow **salt-pan** evaporation flats behind a **dyke and breakwall** north of town (hence "Pale-water"). The party stopped here on **Day 3** of the voyage aboard **[The Thornbill](locations/the-thornbill.md)** and found the town **silenced**.

## The Silence (what the party found)
Palewater appeared **abandoned** — but it was not. The whole town had **walked out to the salt pans** and locked into a **mass ritual/trance**, ~1 day before the party arrived.

- **No life, no torches, most doors standing open**; a **thin, even layer of salt over everything** (the town's own trade good — and, it turns out, tied to the ritual)
- **Instantaneous, simultaneous event, ~1 day ago:** uneaten (un-rotted) food, standing water not yet evaporated, the harbormaster's **quill frozen mid-word**, tools and personal effects **dropped mid-task**. Everyone stopped in the same heartbeat.
- **Raker touched the sea on approach and recoiled** — an eerie, *wrong* sensation. The bay water is the ritual's medium.
- Advised (by Merrin/Onna) to leave town north via **the Drover's Road**.

## The Echoes (and the naming mechanic — KEY REVEAL)
Some of the silenced left **"Echoes"** — spectral remnants **anchored in reflective surfaces** (a mirror, a window, water) at the spot they were taken. Echoes lash out because they are **trapped**, not evil.

- **Speaking an Echo's true name FREES it** — it departs at peace, saying **"freedom,"** and dissolves into salt that vanishes. Danior discovered this by naming the smith's Echo **"Corum."**
- Fighting/shattering an Echo's anchor instead **destroys** it (as happened to [Dessa Kell](npcs/dessa-kell.md)'s echo at the harbormaster's mirror — killed before her name was known).
- **The party freed all of Palewater's Echoes** building-by-building using names gathered from the town's records/objects: **[Corum](npcs/corum.md)** (smith), **[Hen](npcs/hen.md)** (potboy — *"freedom is sweet; but there are more"*), a **father and his children**, the **bakery**, and others (~half a dozen+). Per Hazik's read, **none remain.**
- **Significance:** this is a **release/relief mechanic for the silenced** — a possible lead for the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)** question of whether the Hollowing can be **reversed**. Caveat: this is *release to peace* (for the dead), **not confirmed restoration to life**.

## The Living Ritual (the cause — unresolved)
North over the dyke/breakwall, on the salt pans, the party found **~200 townsfolk standing in shin-deep salt water, arranged in a pattern**, silent, heads bowed.

- **They are ALIVE, not Hollowed** — a Medicine check showed **slow, shallow pulse, breathing/pulsing *as one*** (synchronized), **wide-eyed but entranced.** They still have their eyes (unlike the [Hollowed Woman](npcs/hollowed-woman.md)). **Potentially savable if the ritual is broken.**
- **The center:** **12 figures** around a **stone-looking substance**, behind a **"final wall"** the party could not breach.
- **The 200 act as a non-violent soft barrier:** when Danior (invisible) bumped a few, they **calmly relocated him back to the outer circle** and resumed — no alarm, no violence.
- **Scouting:** Raker Wild Shaped into a **cat**; Danior used a **Potion of Invisibility**. They reached the inner ring but **could not get over the final wall** to the center.

## The Reversal — Garrow, the Two Rites, and the Breaking (session climax)
The "conscious man" who lit two torches was **[Inquisitor-Scholar Teodric Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)** — the sole waking person in Palewater, and the ritual's **architect.**

- **His confession:** Garrow, a loyal Penance **Inquisitor-Scholar**, had spent his life seeking a **"humane"** purification to replace the **pyre**. He performed his **[Rite of Purification](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)** on the townsfolk (counted **180** souls) believing he was **saving** them — but admitted he **could not release them or undo it.** Not malice; **zealotry.**
- **The decode:** Hazik's **[Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)** exposed **invisible-ink text** on Garrow's copy — the hidden **release step (viii, "Of the Breaking")**. The party showed him Mortivar's **[Rite of the Vessel](items/the-rite-of-the-vessel.md)** (recovered from the Candlekeep vault). Side by side, the two documents **decode the Hollowing**: the merciful "keep-and-return" rite was corrupted by **deleting the naming (vi)**, **hiding the release (viii)**, **adding a permanent Sealing (ix)**, stripping anchors, merging all souls into "one pan," and reversing the spiral and tide. The margins gave the practical keys: the ring's seam faces **north-northeast**, and the return rides the **climbing tide**.
- **The erasure:** Garrow doubted the Vessel — *the Crusaders have no documentation of Mortivar at all.* That very absence reads as a **deliberate scrub** (weapon + blame kept, mercy erased). The truth survived only in the Avowed's vault, via **[Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**.
- **Garrow turned** — repentant, *"questioning his entire existence."* He **offered his services**, handed over his **ledger of the 180 names** (with entry order), and **enchanted Das's shortsword** into **[Tidehold](items/tidehold.md)** to fight the souls that resist and turn hostile.
- **The Breaking attempt:** on the **rising tide**, the party worked the salt-pan ring — the **8-foot salt-crusted mortar wall** being the physical form of the sealed ring. They began the **Return**: speaking names and walking souls out one by one; each **vanished saying *"I am released."*** *(Whether this was restoration-to-life or release-to-peace was never confirmed.)*
- **Resisting undead** rose and attacked, as Garrow warned. The party **won the fight** (Tidehold, Danior's Turn Undead, and his custom **Prayer of Healing** granting short-rest benefits mid-fight).
- **The flood cut it short.** The climbing tide **broke in over the pans**, forcing the party to **abandon the ritual before finishing.** Most souls were released, but **~23 did not make it out** of the remaining ritual — **fate unknown** (lost, drowned, or still bound).
- **Garrow did not make it out.** He was **lost in the commotion/flood** — **fate unknown, not confirmed dead.**

## Key Locations in Town
- **The Concordance** — a **church**; the name of the *building* (not the faith). Practices the **[Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md)' monotheistic religion** (mainstream faith; name unknown). Altar holds **tiered memorial tokens** ("tributes/remembrances"). One token — **"for E. G. who burned"** (a fire death, identity unknown) — was **barely salt-dusted**, meaning someone **handled it *after* the silence** (the salt man?). Danior kept it (polished precious stone). The church itself held **no anchored Echoes.**
- **Harbormaster's office** — desk of **[Dessa Kell](npcs/dessa-kell.md)**; ledger of ship traffic (last entry ~1 day ago); her worn canvas coat bore a **fine, non-magical brass ship pin** (a valued personal token). Her Echo was in the **mirror** (destroyed, not freed).
- **The forge** — smith **[Corum](npcs/corum.md)** (maker's mark on a horseshoe); iron-working tools, forge **still warm** (extinguished ~1 day ago). His Echo was in the **window** (freed by name).
- **The net yard** — fishing-gear storage; nets in disarray, standing water. A **netmender's** grief-keepsake: **a wedding ring strung on net-thread**, inscribed **"my dear Mera."** Likely the **"fish man who walked into the ocean"** — a widower who walked into the sea when the silence came.
- **The inn** — home of the recurring **[grey cat](npcs/the-grey-cat.md)**; **[Hen](npcs/hen.md)** the potboy's Echo was in a kitchen water-reflection (freed).

## Significance
A **[Penance Crusader](factions/penance-crusaders.md)-faith town** hit by a **mass silencing** — the corrupted rite (the Hollowing) or a related ritual turned on the Crusaders' *own* people. The living, savable 200 and the Echo-freeing mechanic both bear directly on the party's central quest: **can the Hollowing be reversed?**
