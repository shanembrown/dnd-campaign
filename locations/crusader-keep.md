# Crusader Keep

## Description
The only [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md) stronghold in [Wilden](locations/the-groveborne.md).

## Significance
- Ships depart from here to reach the Penance Crusaders' capital on [Forden](locations/forden.md)
- [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) was adamant the party should travel here with him

## Notable Locations Within
- **The Stockade** — keep's holding cells; dark, cramped. **Antimagic field active** — Hazik attempted Light and the spell failed; something in the air is suppressing magic. Party currently imprisoned here.

## Notes
- Party arrived at Crusader Keep after burying [Finn](npcs/finn-and-creel.md) and the wounded Crusader rider near [Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md)
- [Inquisitor Krell](npcs/inquisitor-krell.md) did not believe their story
- Party stripped of weapons and items, locked in the Stockade while Krell sends guards to verify their account
- Party is carrying sensitive intel: [Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md)'s death, the rider's betrayal, and the abandonment of Wicks Hollow — unclear who in the Crusaders can be trusted, and unclear whether the verification guards will return loyal or compromised
- **No rations, no contact** during imprisonment — long enough for the party to sleep at least once with no guard interaction. Suggests neglect, deliberate isolation, or chaos within the keep
- Das discovered his cell door's hinge was loose; party committed to breaking out
- **Keys to the cells are hanging outside the cell doors** — impossible in a functioning stockade. Either deliberate (trap, setup, or covert ally), or the keep is in chaos / abandoned
- Das used the keys to release the party; a guard came down the stairs and combat began
- **Party fought and defeated 4 guards and a mastiff** during the breakout
- **Gear recovered** — weapons, spellbook, holy symbol, [Broken Silver Coin](items/broken-silver-coin.md), [Black Iron Nail](items/black-iron-nail.md), [Telepathy Rings](items/telepathy-rings.md), [Morwen's Recipe Book](items/morwen-recipe-book.md), [Medallion of Thoughts](items/medallion-of-thoughts.md)
- Gear was stored in a room down the hallway from the cells, still within the Stockade level. Party is currently in that room — session paused here
