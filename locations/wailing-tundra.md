# Wailing Tundra

## Description
A cold, unrelenting frozen expanse beyond [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md). Believed to house no life.

## Features
- Only accessible by ship (a journey of many days)
- Harsh, lifeless atmosphere
- Ruins of an ancient civilization that once inhabited the region before dispersing

## Encounters


## Significance
Groveborn Rangers are reporting dark, foul magic emanating from this region - possibly connected to the resurgence of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md)'s necromancy.
