# The Warm Ash

An **inn inside [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)**, where **[Gudrun](npcs/gudrun.md)** works.

The party is **seeking it out** — Gudrun's name was given to them at the inn in the town a day west on the Drover's Road, as **someone who might know more about [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**.

## The Name
**"The Warm Ash"** — in a city where **every forehead carries an ash thumbprint**, stalls sell **"salt of ashes... for a cleaner tomorrow"**, and **pyre smoke stands over the rooftops.**

*Warm* ash is **fresh** ash — from a fire that has only just gone out.

In Crusivar the name reads as ordinary piety, which may be exactly the point: an unremarkable name in a city of ash is good cover for a house that is not unremarkable.

## Why It Matters
The party's live question is **whose lock the unmarked [Avowed Brass Key](items/avowed-brass-keys.md) opens** — a key made by the same craftsman as Ennic's gifts, recovered from the Candlekeep vault, with no markings and no known door.

An inn in the capital, staffed by someone who knew Ennic, is a plausible answer.
