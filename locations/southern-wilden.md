# Southern Wilden

## Description
A small, desolate fishing village. Part of [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md), near the edge of known lands. Huts with grass roofs.

## Features
- Small village, not many residents
- Grass-roofed huts
- Evidence of fishing activity

## Encounters
- Party met [Lightkeeper Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) of the [Order of the Sentinel Guards](factions/order-of-the-sentinel-guards.md) here

## Significance
[Reif](npcs/reif.md) suggested the party investigate this area as a starting point toward the [Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md).

## Notes
[Istren Vale](npcs/istren-vale.md) retreated here after his party encountered overwhelming forces in the [Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md) — he was the only survivor.
