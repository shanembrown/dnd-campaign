# The Waystation

## Description
A stopping point on the **Drover's Road**, **less than a day's travel from [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)**. The party camped here with **[Jory Tinwhistle](npcs/jory-tinwhistle.md)'s Travelling Wonder Show** on the way east.

**There is an audience** — **simple people who work hard and enjoy entertainment.** The show performs here.

## Why It Matters — The Dress Rehearsal
This is the party's **first performance**, and it lands at exactly the right moment: **a low-stakes crowd, one day out from the Crusader capital.**

- **The cover gets tested here or it gets tested in Crusivar.** Better to find the holes in front of farmhands than in front of **[Inquisitor Marrek](npcs/inquisitor-marrek.md)**.
- **Stage names must hold.** Raker = **The Lamplighter** · Das = **The Trickster** · Hazik = **The Great Mysterio** · Danior = **The Titan from the Tundra**. A slip in front of this crowd is a free lesson; a slip at the gate is not.
- **The acts:** Raker pivoted to **Produce Flame** (a cantrip — unlimited, so the act is repeatable without spending Wild Shape's 2 uses).
- **This crowd is also a source.** Working people one day from the capital know the road, the gates, who's been searching travellers lately, and what the mood in Crusivar is. **A performer can ask questions a stranger cannot.**

## AMBUSH — The Circle Finds the Party
As the show was **wrapping up** (well received), **the crowd went silent** and **8 shadowy figures appeared behind them**: **7 Skeletons + 1 Cult Fanatic.**

- **These are [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) forces** — the party recognised them as **the same creature types fought before**, notably at the **[Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple** (Xellath, Zaelar, cultists, skeletons, Wights).
- **A Cult Fanatic does not wander.** This is a **targeted attack**, not a random road encounter — which means **the party's cover was broken, or they were tracked.**
- **Eight figures.** Note the recurring number: **[Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)'s riddle** (*"eight bone pieces; the chain circles around"*), the **8-tile vault lock** at Candlekeep, the altar requiring **8 [telepathy rings](items/telepathy-rings.md)**. Eight is a Circle number.

### How Did They Find the Party?
The party was **in costume, under stage names, one day from Crusivar.** Candidates:
1. **Someone talked** — the Waystation crowd, the road, or within Jory's show.
2. **Tracked from Palewater** — where the party very publicly undid a Hollowing.
3. **[The Oracle](npcs/the-oracle.md)'s unresolved fortune:** *"a button will betray you within the hour."* **[Gertrude](npcs/jory-tinwhistle.md) prompted [Danior](pcs/danior.md) to press a button, and "nothing happened."* The owl has been **3 for 3** on every testable prediction. **A button that summons something is a button that appears to do nothing.**

## Cautions
- **A day out from Crusivar means Crusader presence is plausible** — patrols, tax men, pilgrims, or off-duty soldiers in the crowd.
- The party is carrying the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)**, both **[rites](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)**, Eldrix's sealed **"M" scroll**, the **[Deck of Many Things](items/deck-of-many-things.md)**, and the **[Avowed Brass Keys](items/avowed-brass-keys.md)**.
- **[The Oracle](npcs/the-oracle.md)'s** unresolved fortune — *"a button will betray you within the hour"* — is still outstanding, and the owl has been **3 for 3** on everything testable.
