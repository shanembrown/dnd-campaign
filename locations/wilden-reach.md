# Wilden Reach

## Description
A small cluster of islands to the southwest of [Wilden](locations/the-groveborn.md), accessible by boat from [Southreach](locations/southreach.md). South of here lies [The Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md) island.

## Features
- Accessible by boat from Southreach
- Forested/jungle terrain
- Previously believed uninhabited by [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md)
- A torchlit entrance discovered at dusk leading to a cultist outpost

## Encounters
- Found [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md) on one of the islands — frightened, asked party for help urgently
- Defeated cultists at a torchlit entrance
- Defeated a Steam Mephit behind a door; two more Steam Mephits defeated in an adjacent room

## Significance
[Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md) recently borrowed a ship from a local fisherwoman in Southreach and headed here on a quest for good.
