# Wilden Reach

## Description
A small cluster of islands to the southwest of [Wilden](locations/the-groveborn.md), accessible by boat from [Southreach](locations/southreach.md). South of here lies [The Wailing Tundra](locations/wailing-tundra.md) island.

## Features
- Accessible by boat from Southreach
- Forested/jungle terrain
- Previously believed uninhabited by [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md)
- A torchlit entrance discovered at dusk leading to a cultist outpost

## Wilden Reach Temple (Cultist Outpost)
- Symmetrical temple layout
- Two secret passages flanking the temple, opened with the passphrase "Klaatu barada nikto"
- Cultists inside performing a summoning incantation — similar dark magic to [Featherthorne Hollow](locations/featherthorne-hollow.md)
- Skeletons found guarding rooms — more undead tied to the Circle's necromancy

## Encounters
- Found [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md) on one of the islands — frightened, asked party for help urgently
- Defeated cultists at a torchlit entrance
- Defeated a Steam Mephit behind a door; two more Steam Mephits defeated in an adjacent room
- Ambushed and defeated a lone cultist separated from the ritual group
- Fought skeletons in a room opposite the ambushed cultist
- Found a [Medallion of Thoughts](items/medallion-of-thoughts.md) in a chest (given to [Danior](pcs/danior.md))
- **Major battle**: Party fought Xellath, Zaelar (named cultists), 2 additional cultists, 2 skeletons, and 2 Wights (summoned via ritual). Hardest fight yet — multiple party members went down. Zaelar killed; Xellath knocked unconscious and captured alive for interrogation. Party barely survived.

## Named Cultists
- **Xellath** — powerful cultist spellcaster, capable of Hold Person and summoning Wights. Captured alive.
- **Zaelar** — named cultist. Killed in battle.

## Significance
[Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md) recently borrowed a ship from a local fisherwoman in Southreach and headed here on a quest for good.

## Notes

- Xellath is captured and unconscious — party plans to interrogate using [Medallion of Thoughts](items/medallion-of-thoughts.md)
