# Sigil of the Occulet

## Description
A sigil associated with the [Cult of the Occulet](../factions/cult-of-the-occulet.md).

## Obtained
Taken from dark occultists (earlier in the campaign).

## Significance
The same markings were found on bandits encountered in [Fangorne Mountain Overlook](../locations/fangorne-mountain-overlook.md), suggesting a connection between the bandits and the cult.
