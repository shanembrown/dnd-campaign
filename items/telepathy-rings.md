# Telepathy Rings

## Description
Rings worn by cultists of the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) that enable telepathic communication among wearers. The wearer hears a cacophony of voices — a distinct signal separate from their own thoughts. With concentration, individual voices can be isolated, but the languages spoken appear to be Abyssal or other unknown tongues.

## Effects
- Wearer enters a trance-like state
- Hears the thoughts/voices of other ring wearers
- Can attempt to narrow down and isolate individual voices
- Languages heard are not understood by the party (likely Abyssal)
- **Dangerous**: Knocked [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md) unconscious on contact — possibly rejects non-cultists or those aligned with good/nature
- Hazik was able to wear it without the violent reaction
- If the party can hear the cultists, the cultists may be able to hear them — party is keeping the rings off for now

## Recovery
- The [divinity stone](items/sigil-of-the-occult.md) can revive those knocked unconscious by the rings (touching it to their chest)

## Connection to the Altar
- 8 rings total are needed for the altar at the [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple
- The altar has 4 colored skulls (blue, green, red, black) surrounding a central skull
- Party currently has 2 rings

## Obtained
Recovered from cultists at the [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple.

## Notes
- Party plans to keep them off until they can understand Abyssal
