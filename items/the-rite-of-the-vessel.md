# The Rite of the Vessel

## What It Is
The **original, merciful** purification rite — *"that the self be kept through harm, and afterward returned."* Recovered from the never-opened, magic-locked shelf in the **[Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md)** library vault (unlocked via Ennic's *"eight bone pieces; the chain circles around"* riddle). Bears the stylized **"M" sigil** of **[Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)** at the foot of the page.

This is the **uncorrupted** counterpart to Garrow's **[Rite of Purification](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)**. Compared side by side, the difference between the two **is the Hollowing**.

## Purpose
To **temporarily shelter a soul** through unavoidable harm and then **return it whole** — a protective act, not a removal. *"The rite keeps; it does not take."*

## The Seven Steps
1. **Of the Need** — when harm comes the flesh cannot flee, the self is **carried aside and kept** (as grain is kept from the storm).
2. **Of the Salt** — draw **living salt of the subject's own tears**; shape **one vessel for each self**. *No vessel shall hold two. Fresh tears only — old grief will not bind.*
3. **Of the Ground** — lay the spiral **sunwise**, winding **without → within**; the path is walked **inward, toward keeping**.
4. **Of the Hour** — begin at **moonrise on the climbing tide**. *What the water lifts, the water may return.*
5. **Of the Anchors** — each walker bears a **token of their own keeping** (a thing loved, handled, of home). *The self follows what it knows.*
6. **Of the Walking** — walk with them one by one, unhurried; **speak their names at the turning of each ring**. *Seven in one turning, no more. Haste breaks the thread.*
7. **Of the Return** — when the danger is passed, **break the ring where it stands open** and **call them out as they went in, last to first**. The self returns along the salt; *what the salt keeps, the salt shall yield.*

## The Diagrams (margin illustrations)
Four diagrams down the right edge encode the rite's mechanics — and mirror the corrupted **[Rite of Purification](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)** exactly in reverse:
1. **Spiral** — *sunwise, from without to within*, drawn with a **sun symbol** (walked inward, tied to daylight/rising).
2. **The ring** — drawn with a **visible gap**: *"the ring stands open — the door is left open,"* with the note **_"the gap faces north-northeast."_** ⬅️ **The seam location.** This is where the ring is left open so souls can be called back out — and therefore where a *sealed* ring must be forced open to Break it.
3. **Tide** — wavy lines + **UP arrow**: *"moonrise · the climbing tide"* (the return is a **flood-tide** act — *what the water lifts, the water may return*).
4. **Vessel shape** — a single **teardrop**: *"one vessel for each — no vessel shall hold two."*

**Sigil:** the jagged stylized **"M" of Mortivar** (a *personal* mark — vs. the orthodox **Penance cross** on the corrupted copy, which dresses the Hollowing as Church-sanctioned).

## Footer (the warning)
> *"The rite keeps; it does not take. A door that cannot be opened again is not a door. Remember this when others forget it."*

Read as a deliberate warning from the author against **sealing** the rite permanent (the exact corruption in the Purification version).

## Significance
- **The cure the party sailed for.** This is the intact method to **release** souls kept in salt — applicable to the **180 living townsfolk** in the Palewater pans, and the model for reversing the **Hollowing** (cure for **Draffy** / the **Hollowed Woman**).
- **Step 6 "Of the Walking" = the [Echo-naming](npcs/hen.md) mechanic** the party discovered independently at Palewater (speaking a soul's true name frees it). Technique specified: **seven per ring-turning, unhurried.**
- **Signed "M" — Mortivar authored the *merciful* rite.** Reframes the antagonist: his original invention was a **shelter-and-return** mercy, later corrupted into the Hollowing (the copy in **[Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)**'s hand). Possible that Mortivar fell and betrayed his own work, or was blamed for a corruption others made.
- **Open question:** is this document the sealed **"M" scroll** from the vault (now readable), the vault's **black leather pad**, or a separate recovered page? Confirm with DM.
