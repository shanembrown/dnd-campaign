# The Deck of Many Things

## Description
A legendary, wildly dangerous magic item. Came in a rather large leather-bound box with a **flap on top**, given to **[Raker](pcs/raker.md)** by **[Sehir Vondrel](npcs/sehir-vondrel.md)** below decks aboard **[The Thornbill](locations/the-thornbill.md)**. Holds **13 large cards**. Identified by **[Hazik](pcs/hazik.md)** via an Arcana check.

## What It Is
- One of the most powerful and **most dangerous** items in the game. Drawing a card invokes an immediate, often irreversible magical effect — some are great boons, others are catastrophic.
- **This is a custom/expanded deck.** Per Hazik's Arcana read, the **full set is 66 cards**, and the party holds only **13 of them**. (Not the standard 13- or 22-card deck — the DM is running a larger version.)
- **The party has no way to know which 13 of the 66 they hold** — so they can't rule out any outcome. Every draw is blind.
- **53 cards are unaccounted for**, out in the world somewhere. Strong potential campaign hook: who holds the rest, where they are, and whether collecting (or keeping them apart) matters.
- The bearer declares **how many cards** they intend to draw *before* drawing; they must draw that many within 1 hour. Drawing fewer than declared is a bad idea (the deck holds you to it / unused draws can trigger automatically depending on the version).
- Effects range from gaining wishes, levels, or treasure, to losing your soul, being imprisoned in another plane, summoning an avatar of death, or having a beloved NPC turn against you.

## Status
- **In Raker's possession**, carried in secret
- Sehir told Raker he is **"not to speak of her"** — the deck and its source are to be kept quiet
- **No cards drawn yet**

## Notes
- Sehir is a suspected **seer/dreamer** who has "dreamt of Raker many times" — gifting this to Raker specifically is almost certainly tied to whatever she has foreseen. Why she'd hand a party this particular item is a major open question (test of fate? a tool she's seen Raker needing? a burden?)
- **Handle with extreme care.** This is the kind of item that can rewrite a campaign in a single draw — discuss as a party before anyone draws a single card

## Open Questions
- Why give *this* to Raker, and why the secrecy?
- Does Sehir intend for the party to use it, or merely to carry/protect it?
- **Where are the other 53 cards of the 66-card set?** Who holds them? Is this a fragment of something larger Sehir (or someone) is assembling?
