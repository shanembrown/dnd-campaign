# The Reader's Lamp

## Description
A lamp that **requires no flame or fuel** — it lights with a **click**. A family heirloom of [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md)'s line, inherited from the person who raised/trained **[Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**, and passed to Hazik on Ennic's deathbed.

## Properties
- **Translates any text** into a language the bearer understands, within its **5 ft radius glow**
- Lights as an **action**
- Can be lit for **1 hour per long rest**
- No flame or fuel required

## Significance
- **Solves the party's central obstacle** — they came to [Candlekeep](locations/candlekeep.md) to learn/read **Abyssal**. The Lamp lets [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) read the party's **Abyssal Hollowing document** and any future Abyssal inscriptions himself.
- **Text only** — it does *not* translate the *spoken* voices of the [Telepathy Rings](items/telepathy-rings.md) or the bone whistle.
- Makes the party **less dependent on the compromised Avowed leadership** ([Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)) for translation — they can read sensitive documents privately.

## Notes
- Ration the hour: 5 ft radius, action to light, 1 hr per long rest. Save it for high-value Abyssal material.
- Thematic note: the true "Reader" legacy passes to Hazik even as the institutional **Reader** (Sevrin) has gone dark.
