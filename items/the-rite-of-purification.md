# The Rite of Purification

## What It Is
The **corrupted** purification rite found on **[Inquisitor-Scholar Teodric Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)**'s desk at **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)** — *"that the flesh be made clean, and the flock be made one."* **Transcribed "after the hand of [Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)."** Marked with a **cross sigil** (Penance faith).

This is the **weaponized inversion** of Mortivar's **[Rite of the Vessel](items/the-rite-of-the-vessel.md)**. It is the **Hollowing**, written out as a procedure. Garrow believed it a *humane* purification and used it on the **180 townsfolk** now entranced in the salt pans — **not realizing it was the corruption.**

## Purpose (as corrupted)
To **cleanse the "corruptible self" away** and merge the many into **"one flock,"** then **seal it permanent.** Where the Vessel *keeps and returns*, this *yields and seals*.

## The Nine Steps (two separate sabotages)
The corruption attacks the rite from **both ends**:
- **Step vi is DELETED** — the written page jumps v → vii. The naming step ("Of the Walking") is gone entirely; Garrow never had it.
- **Step viii "Of the Breaking" — the RELEASE — was HIDDEN in invisible ink.** This is the step the **[Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)** exposed. The permanence-step **ix "Of the Sealing" was left visible**; the *way out* was the part concealed. Garrow knew how to lock souls in but never that there was a way to free them — which is exactly why he told the party he **"doesn't know how to release them."** Seeing the hidden release revealed is what made him go gaunt: the escape existed all along, deliberately kept from him.

1. **Of the Concordance** — *(added framing)* the corruptible self is set aside, that the flesh be made clean.
2. **Of the Need** — the self is set aside and **yielded** (as **chaff** is yielded from grain). *(Vessel: **kept**, as grain kept *from* the storm.)*
3. **Of the Salt** — **the pan entire** is the vessel of **the many**; "one keeping, one salt, one flock." *(Vessel: **one vessel per person**, from their own tears — here the selves are **merged**.)*
4. **Of the Ground** — spiral **widdershins**, winding **within → without**; walked **outward, toward cleansing**. *(Reversed from the Vessel.)*
5. **Of the Hour** — begin at **moonset on the ebbing tide**; *what the water lowers, the water carries away.* *(Reversed — no return.)*
6. **Of the Walking** — ⬅️ **DELETED (absent from the page).** In the Vessel this is *speak their names at each ring's turning* — the individual thread back. The corrupted rite omits it, which is why the 180 collapsed into "one flock" and **cannot be called back one by one.** *This is the piece the party independently rediscovered as the [Echo-naming](npcs/hen.md).*
7. **Of the Yielding** — *"let them carry nothing; what the self clings to, the cleansing cannot loose."* *(Inverts the Vessel's **Anchors** — the token of home is **stripped**.)*
8. **Of the Breaking** — ⬅️ **HIDDEN in invisible ink; revealed by the Reader's Lamp.** Break the open ring, call them out last-to-first; *what was taken with harm, the salt returns.* **This is the release** — and it was concealed from the practitioner, which is why Garrow could not free the 180 (his own words).
9. **Of the Sealing** — *(visible to Garrow)* *"Seal the ring entire, that the setting-aside be made permanent."* **The Hollowing act** — the "door that cannot be opened again" the Vessel warns against. Left **visible** while the release was hidden, so a practitioner would learn only to lock souls in.

## The Diagrams (margin illustrations)
Four diagrams down the right edge mirror the **[Rite of the Vessel](items/the-rite-of-the-vessel.md)** in reverse:
1. **Spiral** — *widdershins, from within to without*, **no sun** (walked outward; the daylight/rising association stripped).
2. **The ring** — drawn **closed and complete**: *"the ring sealed entire"* (no gap). Contrast the Vessel's ring, which stands **open at north-northeast** — **that NNE bearing is where a sealed ring must be forced open to Break it.**
3. **Tide** — wavy lines + **DOWN arrow**: *"moonset · the ebbing tide"* (*the water carries away* — no return). The **reversal/Breaking wants the opposite: the climbing tide at moonrise.**
4. **Vessel shape** — a wide **open pan with many dots**: *"the pan entire — the vessel of the many"* (the mass-merger — 180 selves in one pan, why they read as "one flock").

**Sigil:** an orthodox **circle-with-cross (the Penance faith cross)** — dressing the corrupted rite as **Church-sanctioned**, vs. the *personal* **"M" of Mortivar** on the merciful original. Fits the pattern: the Hollowing was **institutionalized under a veneer of orthodoxy** and handed to devout practitioners as approved mercy.

## The Binding — the "First 12" (Garrow's account)
The **12 figures around the stone substance** at the ritual's core (behind the "final wall" the party couldn't breach) are Garrow's **"first 12"** — the earliest souls he processed, and they **bind the whole ritual together.** They are the keystone that makes the 180 pulse/breathe **as one** ("one flock").

- **Possible elegant release:** if the 12 are the threads holding the flock, **loosing the 12 keystones may unravel the binding and release the 180** — without needing all 180 individual names. Hinges on whether **Garrow knows his first 12 by name** (his earliest subjects — the ones he'd most likely remember). If so, the **naming bottleneck drops from 180 → 12.**
- **Sequencing tension:** *"call them out as they went in, last to first."* The 12 went **first**, so by that rule they come out **last** — the 180 released before the 12. Pulling a binding-anchor out of order risks **collapse/backlash.** *Confirm the correct sequence with Garrow.*
- **Grim unknowns:** the 12 are the longest-processed, wrapped around the **stone substance** — they may be **partly Hollowed already** and serving as anchors. Open: are the 12 still savable, or the price of saving the 180? Is the **stone substance the sealed vessel** ("the pan entire… made permanent") — and does breaking it **release** or **destroy**? Who are the 12 (willing acolytes vs. chosen victims)?

## Practical Reversal Intel (from the diagrams)
- **The seam is NORTH-NORTHEAST.** To Break a sealed ring, force it open at the NNE bearing (where the Vessel leaves the door open).
- **Time the Breaking to the climbing tide / moonrise** — the reverse of the ebb Garrow sealed on. *"What the water lifts, the water may return."*

## The Corruption, Summarized — Every Exit Sealed Off
- **Delete the naming (vi):** no individual thread → souls merged into "one flock," uncallable.
- **Hide the release (viii)** in invisible ink, while leaving the **Sealing (ix) visible** → the practitioner learns only how to **lock souls in permanent**, never how to free them.
- Plus the inversions along the way (self **yielded** not kept; **one pan** not one-vessel-each; spiral & tide **reversed**; anchors **stripped**). It is the **[Rite of the Vessel](items/the-rite-of-the-vessel.md)** run **backward with the doors hidden** — and handed to a believer as a mercy. Engineered to make a devout man a Hollower without his knowing.

## Crucial Implication — Garrow Likely Already SEALED the 180
**Garrow told the party he COMPLETED the Rite of Purification on the 180 within the last day.** Because his copy **hid the Breaking (viii)** and left the **Sealing (ix) visible**, "completing" the rite as he knew it means he **performed the Sealing** — believing it the merciful end. **The 180 are very likely sealed.**

Countervailing evidence that a window remains:
- **Medicine check:** the 180 are **alive, NOT Hollowed, "potentially savable"** — slow pulse, breathing as one, entranced but present.
- **The ritual site is still active** — the **12 figures + stone substance** behind the "final wall"; something is *ongoing*, not finished-and-gone.
- The seal is **< 1 day old.**

Read together: **Sealing makes the setting-aside *permanent*, but the hollowing-out may still be *completing*** — a **closing window, not a shut door** — or the **seal itself is now the thing to break.** Diagram tension: the corrupted ring is drawn **"sealed entire"** (no opening), while Breaking says *"break the ring **where it stands open**"* → a sealed ring may have to be **forced back open at the seam** before it can be broken.

**The reversal the party holds:** the Lamp exposed the **Breaking (viii)** and the party already re-discovered the deleted naming (vi) as the **[Echo-naming](npcs/hen.md)** — together, every step needed to release the 180. **It is now a race:** break the ring and walk them out (by name, last-to-first, seven per turning) **before the hollowing settles permanent.**

**Open questions for Garrow:** when exactly he sealed it; whether "completed" means *irreversible* or *sealed-and-settling*; whether the **12 + stone substance** is the sealed anchor; and whether the sealed ring can still be broken, and **where the seam is.**

## Saving the 180 (the practical cure)
1. **Do NOT let the ring be sealed (ix)** — sealing = permanent Hollowing.
2. **Break the ring where it stands open (viii).**
3. **Walk them out by name, last-to-first, seven per turning, unhurried** ("haste breaks the thread") — the **[Echo-naming](npcs/hen.md)** at scale.
4. **Return their anchors** where possible so each self "follows what it knows."

## Reversal Procedure — Working Plan (grafting the Vessel's Return onto the sealed rite)
The **[Rite of the Vessel](items/the-rite-of-the-vessel.md)** and the Purification are the **same rite** — the Purification is the Vessel with the *keeping* half done and the *return* half deleted (vi) / hidden (viii). Reversing it means **finishing it correctly**: running the Vessel's **Return** onto Garrow's sealed ring so the salt *yields* instead of *keeps*. **DM to adjudicate specifics.**

1. **Time it to the climbing tide / moonrise.** Garrow sealed on the *ebb* ("carries away"); the Return runs on the *flood* ("what the water lifts, the water may return"). The tide turning ebb→flood is the reversal window.
2. **Break the seal at the NNE seam.** The Vessel's ring stands open at **north-northeast**; the Purification "sealed the ring entire." Force it open there (step viii, "break the ring where it stands open").
3. **Re-individuate the merged "flock."** The rite stripped anchors and poured all into one pan. Restore each soul's **anchor object** (a thing loved/handled/of home) so "the self follows what it knows" — anchors + names un-merge the flock.
4. **Supply the naming (vi) — Echo-naming at scale.** Speak each name at the turning of each ring, **seven per turning, unhurried** ("haste breaks the thread").
5. **Call them out last-to-first.** Later 180 first, the **binding first-12 last** — *unless Garrow says the 12 must be loosed first.* (Sequencing = the crux.)
6. **Never re-seal — leave the door open.**

**Assets in hand:** release step (Lamp-revealed viii) ✅ · naming method (Echo) ✅ · **180 names + order (Garrow's ledger)** ✅ · seam (NNE) ✅ · timing (flood tide) ✅ · officiant (Garrow co-officiates — he walked them in) ✅
**Still open:** recover **anchor objects** from homes (ledger helps locate) · the **first 12 + stone substance** (savable? released first or last?) · asserting *"the danger is passed"* so the Return "takes."

### Outcome (attempted at Palewater)
The party **breached the salt-pan ring** on the rising tide and began the Return — walking souls out by name; each **vanished saying *"I am released."*** **Resisting undead** rose and were fought off. But the **climbing tide flooded in and forced the party to abandon the ritual unfinished** — **~23 souls did not make it out**, and **Garrow himself was lost** in the flood (fate unknown). Whether the released souls were **restored to life** or **released to peace** was never confirmed (the party skipped the anchor-objects to beat the tide). **The reversal was a partial success at real cost.**
