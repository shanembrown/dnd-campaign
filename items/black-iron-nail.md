# Black Iron Nail

## Description
A black iron object approximately 12 inches tall, resembling a nail or spike. Warm to the touch.

## Discovery
Found by [Ranger Greenward](npcs/ranger-greenward.md) near the [Wilden Reach](locations/wilden-reach.md) Temple, near an ancient shrine of a crowned female figure with gouged-out eyes.

## Properties
- Warm to the touch
- Abyssal in nature
- Resists Identify spell — became too hot to hold when [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) attempted to identify it, forcing him to break concentration
