# Broken Silver Coin

## Description
A silver coin broken in half — a symbol of the old alliance between [The Groveborne](locations/the-groveborne.md) and the [Penance Crusaders](factions/penance-crusaders.md).

## History
Broken by [Keeper Caelwyn Oakseeker](npcs/caelwyn-oakseeker.md) and **Vael Threnfall** (former Crusader ruler, now deceased) after the 100 Year War, symbolizing the end of their alliance.

## Current Status
Given to the party by the Keeper as proof of their mission to propose reforging the alliance with [Bael Threnfall](npcs/bael-threnfall.md) at [Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md). Presumably the Crusaders hold the other half.
