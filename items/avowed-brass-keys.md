# Avowed Brass Keys

The party now holds **two brass keys** tied to [The Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md), possibly by the **same craftsman** (an Avowed keysmith in [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)'s lineage).

## 1. Vord's Brass Key
- **Large, ornate brass key** with an elaborate **floral bow**; "not for an average lock"
- One of three items the party identified for **[Otto Brassweave](npcs/otto-brassweave.md)**
- Identified by [Hazik](pcs/hazik.md) as **attuned to [Halrick Vord](npcs/halrick-vord.md)** — a former Avowed Brother now in **[Crusivar](locations/crusivar.md)** — and **paired to a magical lock somewhere**
- A natural opener for meeting Vord (returning a piece of his past) and a potential vault-opener

## 2. The Unmarked Vault Key
- A **small unmarked brass key on a slim chain**, found inside the **[Hollowing Ledger](items/the-hollowing-ledger.md)** vault
- **No markings**, but Hazik recognized the **same craftsman's hand** as [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)'s gifts (incl. [The Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md))
- Opens an **unknown lock** — filed with a 200-year secret, so it likely opens part of the same network (a deeper cache, the seal's matching case, or something the ledger references)

## Notes
- Worth **comparing the two keys directly** — if both are by the same maker, the Avowed keysmith lineage ties Vord, Ennic, and the Hollowing vault together
- Ennic was clearly **inside the secret** of the vault
