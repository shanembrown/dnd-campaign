# Tidehold

## What It Is
**[Das](pcs/das.md)'s shortsword**, ritually empowered by **[Inquisitor-Scholar Teodric Garrow](npcs/teodric-garrow.md)** at **[Palewater](locations/palewater.md)**. Garrow imbued it after warning the party that some souls **resist "coming back," turn hostile, and are not entirely corporeal** — Tidehold is his answer to those resisting spirits during a reversal of the **[Rite of Purification](items/the-rite-of-purification.md)**.

Named for the **climbing tide** the Return rides and the rite's line *"what the salt keeps, the salt shall yield."*

## Properties
- **Martial Melee • Vex** (mastery: on a hit, advantage on your next attack against that creature)
- **Proficiency with a Shortsword allows you to add your proficiency bonus to the attack roll for any attack you make with it.**
- **"What the Salt Keeps":** advantage on saving throws against being **frightened** or **possessed by undead**.

## Why It Matters
- **It is now a magic weapon** — the practical key benefit: Das can **land hits on incorporeal / resistant undead** (the hostile, not-fully-corporeal souls Garrow warned of) that a mundane blade would pass through.
- **Anti-fear / anti-possession** — makes Das the party's **front-line anchor** against spirits that would panic or hijack a body mid-ritual. During the Breaking, Das holds the line against resisting souls while the others work the ring, names, and anchors.
- Confirms the **reversal will involve combat** — the release is not purely peaceful.
