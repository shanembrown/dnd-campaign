# The Hollowing Ledger

## Description
A **log book recording every Hollowing case for 200 years** — the [Avowed](factions/the-avowed.md)'s secret institutional record of the ritual. Recovered by the party from a **never-opened, magically-locked shelf** in the Candlekeep library.

## Recovery
- **[Eldra Branch](npcs/eldra-branch.md)** escorted the party to a shelf that had **never been opened**, sealed by a **magic-imbued brass lock**
- The lock was a **closed chain of eight tiles** (each tile's symbol matching the next, "circling around") — solved using **[Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md)**'s cryptic phrase **"eight bone pieces; the chain circles around."** Solution tiles: ◇☆ then ☆○.

## Contents of the shelf
- **A black leather pad/folio**
- **A waxed seal bearing the mark of [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)** — a stylized **"M"** — on a **sealed scroll** (see notes; not yet opened, possibly warded)
- **A small unmarked brass key on a slim chain** — Hazik recognized the **same craftsman** as the gifts from [Brother Ennic](npcs/brother-ennic.md); opens an unknown lock
- **The log book itself** — 200 years of Hollowing cases

## Significance
- **200 years** of cases means the Hollowing **predates [Eldrix Mortivar](npcs/eldrix-mortivar.md)** by far — he is likely the current *master/systematizer* of an old corruption, not its inventor. The Crusaders' "errant creation" may be generations old.
- **Eldrix's seal inside the Avowed's most guarded shelf** implies he has (or had) a direct line into the order — sharpening the **[Reader Sevrin](npcs/reader-sevrin.md)** compromise (the leak may run toward the [Circle](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md) itself).

## Key questions to mine from the ledger
1. **Was any Hollowing ever reversed/cured?** (Critical for **[Draffy](pcs/danior.md)** and the [Hollowed Woman](npcs/hollowed-woman.md).)
2. **"Case zero"** — the earliest entry / the moment the rite was corrupted (likely tied to the crowned-goddess shrine).
3. **[Danior](pcs/danior.md)'s village / Draffy** — is the case logged, did anyone recover?
4. **Recent cases** — [Wicks Hollow](locations/wicks-hollow.md), the Hollowed Woman — is the Avowed leadership still recording (and thus tracking) current activity?
5. **Patterns** — frequency, locations, who performed them.

## Notes
- **Do not break Eldrix's "M" seal blind** — check for Glyph of Warding / alarm / sending first; opening it may alert Eldrix.
- Use **[The Reader's Lamp](items/the-readers-lamp.md)** if any of it is in Abyssal.
- The waxed seal's authority/origin is worth examining (whose secret is this record, really?).
