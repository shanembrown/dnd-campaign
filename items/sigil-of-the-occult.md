# Sigil of the Occult

## Description
A sigil associated with the [Circle of the Occult](factions/circle-of-the-occult.md).

## Obtained
Taken from dark occultists (earlier in the campaign).

## Significance
The same markings were found on members at a camp in [Fangorne Mountain Overlook](locations/fangorne-mountain-overlook.md), confirming their connection to the Circle.
